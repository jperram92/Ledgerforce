# T-0047 — Customer prepayments, deposit invoices and application to final invoice

## Goal
Deposit/prepayment billing format: prepayment receipt or deposit tax invoice to CUSTOMER_PREPAYMENTS, applied on the final invoice.

Every deposit invoice or prepayment receipt carries a **GST supply pattern** that decides when GST
is attributed (accountant-reviewer DL-0280 MAJOR 1, carried here by re-review DL-0285 M6):
`progressive_div156` (each payment is a Div 156 component: GP-012…GP-015), `single_supply` (the
whole GST is attributed at the first payment or invoice, s 29-5(1): GP-012-B/GP-015-B) or
`security_deposit_div99`. Treating every deposit as Div 156 understates 1A (by 700 in the GP-012
example), so a `single_supply` deposit is **refused** until the single-supply posting path exists,
never posted as Div 156.

**Gate (accounting decision).** The `gst_supply_pattern` classification and the
`GST_ATTRIBUTED_UNBILLED` system role are an accounting treatment and data-model decision outside
ADR-0044 (T-0124 open question). They need their own accounting ADR (or DL entry) accepted under
DL-0282 after an accountant-reviewer persona re-review with no BLOCKER. This card may not be
claimed for build until that decision is accepted; write the ADR first (`write-adr`) if nobody has.
