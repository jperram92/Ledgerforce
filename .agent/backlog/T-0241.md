# T-0241 — Cancel an unreleased payment batch

## Goal
T-0207 (RQ-0189 finding 2, ADR-0107) refuses to cancel a bank refund while a live payment batch
pays it, including a draft or partially released batch, because a batch's lines are append-only
and its total is frozen when it is built (migration 0047). The database already allows
`draft → cancelled` and `partially_released → cancelled`, but no module function, route or screen
does it. So a refund (or bill) caught in a draft batch can only be freed by releasing and paying
that batch.

Add `cancelPaymentBatch` (packages/modules/ap) and `POST /ap/payment-batches/:id/cancel`: only a
draft or partially released batch, never a released or confirmed one; with a reason; audited;
Idempotency-Key; permission and SoD step decided with research (who may cancel a batch others
have partly released?). A cancelled batch frees its bills and refunds for the next batch, and the
refund region (T-0207) then offers Cancel again.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC-1 (research) RQ: who may cancel an unreleased batch (maker, releaser, Finance Admin), and
      what happens to partial releases; ADR (public API, SoD)
- [ ] AC-2 (db) a draft or partially released batch is cancelled once (replay); a released or
      confirmed one is refused; its bills and refunds go into the next batch; a refund it held
      can then be cancelled (T-0207)
- [ ] AC-3 (api-db) route, permission, SoD, Idempotency-Key, 404 for another tenant, audit
- [ ] AC-4 (e2e) the payment batch screen offers Cancel for an unreleased batch
