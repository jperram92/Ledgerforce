# T-0048 — Bad debt write-off with GST decreasing adjustment

## Goal
Write off all/part of an invoice to the bad-debt account with 1/11 GST adjustment on accruals basis.

Also the AASB 9 path (accountant-reviewer DL-0280 MAJOR 2, carried here by re-review DL-0285 M6):
when an expected-credit-loss allowance exists, the write-off is made **against the allowance**
(chart 1290, `setting:ar.ecl_allowance_account`, T-0121) and only any excess goes to
`setting:ar.bad_debt_account`. The allowance itself is recognised by an `ar.ecl_provision` source
document. Golden GP-007-A (T-0124) is the expected result; GP-007 stays valid for direct write-off.
