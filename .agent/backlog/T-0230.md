# T-0230 — Slow-upload protection on the Mailgun inbound route

## Goal
Security re-review of T-0215 (N1, Medium) showed that slow uploads can keep `POST /webhooks/mailgun/messages.mime` at 503: the only time limit is the 60 s whole-body timeout, with no minimum rate and no per-client in-flight cap. About 4 trickling connections a minute from one address hold every slot; Mailgun retries a 503 for about 8 hours and then drops the message, so a sustained attack loses supplier bills. Only this route is affected.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC-1 (api-unit) at most 1 upload in flight per client address; a second concurrent upload from the same address is refused (503 + Retry-After) without taking a global slot
- [ ] AC-2 (api-unit) an upload below a minimum rate (e.g. 64 KiB/s averaged after the first 5 s) or with no first byte within a short timeout is aborted with Connection: close and its slot released
- [ ] AC-3 (api-unit) a trickling upload is refused before the 60 s body timeout and a normal delivery afterwards gets 200
- [ ] AC-4 (docs) runbook note: optional Mailgun egress allowlist and an alert on `inbound_busy`
