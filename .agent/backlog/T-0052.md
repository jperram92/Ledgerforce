# T-0052 — Bill approval workflow with SoD

## Goal
Bills route to Approver; entered-by ≠ approved-by above $1,000; small-team mode exceptions.

## Context (design refs)
- Design: §6.5 rule 3, UC-05.
- Specs: [UC-05](../../Docs/specs/UC-05.md) · Register: [success-scenarios.md](../../Docs/specs/success-scenarios.md)

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test + SS id)
- [ ] **SS-UC05-03** (db-integration) — Coded bill routes to Approver; nothing posts before approval. *Given* a coded bill; *when* the AP Clerk submits it; *then* it is Awaiting approval in the Approver queue and no journal exists.
- [ ] **SS-UC05-04** (golden) — Approved bill posts GP-008. *Given* BILL-000045 as in GP-008; *when* the Approver approves it; *then* the journal equals GP-008 and the AP open item is 2,750.00.
- [ ] **SS-UC05-05** (db-integration) — SoD: clerk cannot approve own bill above $1,000. *Given* a $2,750 bill entered by user A; *when* user A approves it; *then* approval is denied with SOD_BILL_SELF_APPROVAL and audited.
- [ ] **SS-UC05-06** (db-integration) — Small-team mode records a visible exception. *Given* small-team mode and a single user; *when* the user approves their own bill; *then* approval succeeds only with a recorded exception that appears on the weekly review report.
