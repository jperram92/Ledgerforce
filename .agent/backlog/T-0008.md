## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test + SS id)
- [ ] **SS-PLAT-04** (db-integration) — RLS isolates tenants on every tenant table. *Given* tenants A and B with data in every tenant-owned table; *when* tenant A selects, aggregates, updates, deletes and inserts with B's ids; *then* zero rows are visible/affected and inserts for B fail; a table without FORCE RLS fails the catalog test.
- [ ] **SS-PLAT-17** (db-integration) — Migrations are forward-only on the previous schema. *Given* the previous release schema with data; *when* current migrations apply; *then* they apply cleanly with no DROP/RENAME.
