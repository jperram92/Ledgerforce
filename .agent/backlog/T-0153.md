# T-0153 — AP release API for the T-0087 screens (split from T-0087)

## Goal
T-0054 and T-0053 built the payment-batch and bank-detail-change commands, but the screens of
T-0087 cannot find what to act on and would show bare ids. Three read-only additions:
- `GET /ap/payment-batches?company_id=` (`ap.read`) lists the company's batches, newest first:
  number, status, payment date, total (decimal string), payment and exclusion counts, releases
  given and required, creator's name.
- `GET /ap/payment-batches/:id?company_id=` additionally names things (additive fields):
  `created_by` and `created_by_name`, `released_by_name` on releases, and on payments and
  exclusions the supplier's `party_name` and the `bill_no`; payments also carry the masked
  `bank_account` (`bsb`, `account_number` = `***` + last 3, design §6.6).
- `GET /bank-detail-changes?company_id=&status=` (`parties.bank_account.read`) lists bank-detail
  changes (default: those awaiting verification) with party name, the new and previous account
  masked, who asked and when, and for decided ones who decided and when it becomes payable.

No migration, no new permission: the tables are already readable by `lf_app` under RLS.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] AC-1 (api-db) `apps/api/src/ap/payment-batch-read.db.test.ts`: the list returns the batch with decimal-string total and counts; the view names suppliers, bills, creator and releaser and masks the account; 403 without `ap.read`; another tenant's batch is 404 / absent from the list; a bad company id is 400.
- [x] AC-2 (api-db) `apps/api/src/parties/bank-detail-change-read.db.test.ts`: a requested change is listed with party name, masked new account and requester's name; the full account number never appears in the body; 403 without `parties.bank_account.read`; another tenant sees nothing; `status=verified` lists the verified one with `payable_from`.
