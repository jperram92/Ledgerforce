# T-0206 — Tax invoice requested on an open single-supply contract (split from T-0203 AC-9 and the AC-3 deposit-invoice refusal, DL-0763)

## Goal

Close ADR-0089 R9 (as extended by addendum A) for documents issued on an open contract. A
recipient who asks for a tax invoice (GST Act s 29-70: the supplier must issue one within 28 days
of a request) gets one. Before any receipt, the document attributes the whole GST with no cash.
After a receipt, it posts nothing and only issues the document. A `single_supply` deposit
invoice on the accruals basis stays refused, with the advice to use GP-021 or the receipt path.

## Open questions (research before code)

- Approve the candidate golden **GP-046** (the next free GP id) under DL-0263, with a research
  record. It needs primary text of s 29-5(1)(b), s 29-70 and the tax-invoice particulars, and a
  comparison with how other systems issue a tax invoice before payment. The candidate is review
  journal J3 (`.agent/evidence/T-0168/accounting-review-20261116.md`):
  Dr GST_ATTRIBUTED_UNBILLED 1,000.00 / Dr CUSTOMER_PREPAYMENTS uncoded 10,000.00 /
  Cr CUSTOMER_PREPAYMENTS coded GST 10,000.00 (tax 1,000.00) / Cr GST collected 1,000.00.
  Expected BAS: G1 11,000.00 / 1A 1,000.00. Later receipts follow GP-040b.

## Acceptance criteria (failing tests to write first)

- AC-1: once GP-046 is Approved, a tax invoice requested on an open contract before any receipt
  posts GP-046 line for line, with the contract dimension on the contract lines. It writes the
  contract's `app.single_supply_attribution` row (one per contract; a concurrent first receipt
  cannot attribute twice). The document carries the tax-invoice particulars and the fixed GST.
- AC-2: the same request after a receipt posts no journal and issues the document showing the
  GST already attributed. A second request re-issues the same document (idempotent).
- AC-3: later receipts after an AC-1 invoice post GP-040b. The receipt reaching the total clears
  the unbilled balance (T-0203 AC-8).
- AC-4 (ADR-0089 R9): a `single_supply` deposit invoice on the accruals basis is refused with
  the advice to use GP-021 or the receipt path, and nothing is written.
