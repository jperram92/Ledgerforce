# T-0195 — Logo, accent colour and Stripe payout accounts

## Goal
T-0186 built the Numbering and billing screen with what wizard step 8 requires (RQ-0167 Q4). Three
optional settings remain without a screen: the invoice logo (PNG/JPEG up to 512 KiB, checked by
magic bytes in `setInvoiceSettings`), the accent colour, and the Stripe payout settings
(`fee_account_id`, `payout_account_id`, `stripe_party_id`; the connector refuses a payout while any
is missing, migration 0060, GP-027).

## Context (design refs)
- Design §4.1 step 8, §3.8; RQ-0167 findings 2 and 4; ADR-0085 (route family); ADR-0071.
- `packages/modules/ar/src/invoice-settings.ts`, `packages/connectors/stripe/src/webhook.ts` (`setStripeSettings`).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC-1 (api-db) logo upload route (body size, content type, magic bytes; 400 in plain words), accent colour on `PUT /setup/billing/invoice` (already accepted by the API) and Stripe payout fields on `PUT /setup/billing/stripe` (supplier party picker), each with Idempotency-Key and audit
- [ ] AC-2 (e2e) the screen shows and changes them; read-only and forbidden states; 1440 and 390, axe
