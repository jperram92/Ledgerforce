# T-0067 — Write-back to Salesforce via sObject Collections from the outbox

## Goal
Outbox events → upsert by LF_Id__c in batches ≤200; field set per §5.4; conflicts logged; queue survives SF outage.

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test + SS id)
- [ ] **SS-UC01-02** (e2e-playwright, screenshot evidence) — Salesforce shows "Draft" within 60 seconds. *Given* SS-UC01-01 has run against a scratch org; *when* 60 seconds elapse; *then* Opportunity.LF_Invoice_Status__c = "Draft" (screenshot of the Opportunity record).
- [ ] **SS-UC01-09** (db-integration) — Salesforce outage does not block accounting. *Given* Salesforce write-back endpoint returns 503; *when* an auto-approved invoice posts; *then* the journal is committed and the write-back stays queued, then succeeds on recovery with one upsert.
- [ ] **SS-UC03-04** (contract) — Salesforce shows Paid with payment date. *Given* SS-UC03-02 processed; *when* write-back runs; *then* the sObject Collections payload sets LF_Invoice__c status Paid with payment date, Opportunity LF_Amount_Paid__c 1,375.00 and Account LF_Balance_Due__c reduced.
- [ ] **SS-UC04-02** (contract) — Credit status "Overdue" written back. *Given* SS-UC04-01 has run; *when* write-back runs; *then* the payload sets Account.LF_Credit_Status__c = "Overdue".
- [ ] **SS-UC11-05** (contract) — Salesforce shows net amount paid. *Given* SS-UC11-03 confirmed; *when* write-back runs; *then* Opportunity.LF_Amount_Paid__c = 1,155.00.
- [ ] **SS-UC12-05** (db-integration) — Salesforce values reconcile to LedgerForce aged AR. *Given* a party with open and overdue invoices; *when* write-back completes; *then* LF_Balance_Due__c and LF_Overdue_Amount__c equal the aged-AR totals for the party.
