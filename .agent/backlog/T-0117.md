# T-0117 — SoD rule for sales-invoice approval (creator ≠ approver)

## Goal
Decide whether a sales invoice may be approved by the user who created it. Design §6.5 lists no
SoD rule for sales invoices and the SoD subject list is closed (`SOD_SUBJECT_STEPS`,
`app.sod_step_known`); T-0040 therefore records the approver but enforces no self-approval rule
(RQ-0104 Q3, DL-0286). If the owner wants one, add subject `invoice` with steps
`create`/`approve`, rule `SOD_INVOICE_SELF_APPROVAL` (condition and threshold to decide), the
database step list, and the API decorator on the T-0116 approve route.

## Context (design refs)
- Design: §6.4 (AR Clerk "create and send invoices"; Approver "cannot create the items they
  approve"), §6.5 (rules 1–6, small-team mode), UC-01 A6 ("awaiting AR Clerk/Approver").
- Code: `packages/shared/src/sod/rules.ts`, migration 0021 (`app.sod_step_known`), T-0015.

## Open questions (→ human gate: security)
- Should invoice approval be SoD-guarded at all, always or above a threshold, and with which
  small-team exception? (security ADR — human gate, AGENTS.md §6)
