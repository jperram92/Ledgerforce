# T-0249 — Cash-basis and nothing-attributed final invoices, final-invoice delivery

Split from T-0231 (DL-0987, RQ-0192 Q8). The research is done: RQ-0192 Q5 (cash basis is
covered by Approved GP-022, DL-0985), Q6 (nothing attributed → ordinary GP-022a invoice that
finalises the contract, DL-0986) and Q7 (delivery event). The criteria below are the T-0231
criteria moved here with their full text; their ids keep the T-0204 numbering.

## Acceptance criteria (failing tests to write first)

- Cash-basis final invoice (ADR-0089 R8): the final invoice of a cash-basis contract posts the
  ordinary invoice and applies the GP-020 receipts by GP-022. Until built it is refused
  CASH_BASIS_NOT_BUILT (T-0204). Build per RQ-0192 Q5: GP-022a (source `ar_invoice`, GST on the
  final price, GP-023a distribution on the printed lines) and GP-022b (source
  `ar_prepayment_application`: Dr CUSTOMER_PREPAYMENTS coded with the receipts' net and negative
  GST on the contract dimension / Dr GST collected / Cr AR), the 22b AR credit allocated to the
  22a item; only receipts not reversed (T-0231) count; golden test against GP-022 line for line.
- Also: a final invoice on an accruals contract with no receipt (NOTHING_ATTRIBUTED) — decided
  in RQ-0192 Q6 / DL-0986: an ordinary GP-022a invoice (GST on the final price, no adjustment
  particulars) that finalises the contract; NOTHING_ATTRIBUTED is lifted.
- Final invoice delivery: the slice-1 final invoice publishes no `ar.invoice.posted` event (no
  e-mail); add it with the Billing To recipients as `approveInvoice` does — for every final-invoice
  path (accruals GP-045, cash GP-022, nothing attributed), refusing NO_BILLING_TO as
  `approveInvoice` does.
- A property (default numRuns) over cash-basis contracts: GST reported per contract equals the
  GST on the final price; CUSTOMER_PREPAYMENTS for the contract ends at zero; AR open = total −
  received.
