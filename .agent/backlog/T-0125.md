# T-0125 — Balance sheet: no offsetting of customer credit balances

## Goal
AASB 101 ¶32 forbids offsetting. When a customer's AR subledger balance is a net credit (for
example GP-005: a credit note on a paid invoice leaves Acme at −220), the balance sheet must not
net it against debit balances in "Trade debtors". At the reporting date the report reclassifies
the sum of net-credit customer balances to a current-liability line ("Customer credit balances").
The mirror case applies to AP: net-debit supplier balances go to a current-asset line ("Supplier
debit balances").

This is presentation only. No journal, no ledger write, and the TB is unchanged. Source: the
accountant-reviewer MINOR on GP-005 (DL-0280, triage DL-0283). It is added to the T-0076 balance
sheet in `packages/modules/reporting`. T-0089 (UI) displays the extra lines; a note has been added
there.
