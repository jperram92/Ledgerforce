# T-0125 — Cash-basis BAS: GST on opening AR/AP items when they are paid

## Goal
For a cash-basis company, GST inside an open item brought across at conversion is attributable when
it is paid after conversion (GST Act s 29-5; RQ-0109 finding 4). The opening journal (GP-037) carries
no tax codes, so `basLabels` (T-0035) attributes nothing for it today. Report G1/1A (sales) and
G11/1B (purchases) in proportion to the cash settled on the item, from its recorded `gst_amount`
(`app.opening_balance_item`); the accruals basis is unchanged (the old system reported it).

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- [ ] AC-1 Given GP-037's items and a 550.00 receipt on INV-1004 in November 2026, the cash-basis BAS
  for the quarter shows G1 550.00 and 1A 50.00 and accruals shows nothing →
  `packages/modules/tax/src/bas.opening.db.test.ts`
