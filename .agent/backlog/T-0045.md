# T-0045 — Customer credit notes

## Goal
Credit note against an invoice (partial/full), proportional GST, approval threshold for AR Clerk, held as credit when invoice already paid.

## Context (design refs)
- Design: §3.8 row 4, UC-11.
- Specs: [UC-11](../../Docs/specs/UC-11.md) · Register: [success-scenarios.md](../../Docs/specs/success-scenarios.md)
- Depends on: T-0040 (Sales invoice lifecycle: draft → approve → post); T-0031 (Golden-posting test runner)

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test + SS id)
- [x] **SS-UC11-01** (golden) — Partial credit note posts GP-005. *Given* paid INV-000123; *when* a $200 ex GST credit note is posted; *then* the journal equals GP-005.
- [x] **SS-UC11-02** (db-integration) — Credit on a paid invoice is held as customer credit. *Given* SS-UC11-01 posted; *when* balances are read; *then* the party has a 220.00 credit and AR subledger = AR control.
- [x] **SS-UC11-06** (db-integration) — Clerk cannot approve own credit note above threshold. *Given* a credit note above the threshold created by the AR Clerk; *when* the same Clerk approves it; *then* approval is denied.
- [x] **SS-UC11-07** (unit) — Credit cannot exceed the creditable amount. *Given* INV-000123 total 1,375.00; *when* a 1,500.00 credit note is attempted; *then* validation fails with CREDIT_EXCEEDS_INVOICE.

- [x] **SS-PLAT-160** (db-integration, added) — lifecycle draft → approved → posted through the engine (ar_credit_note), CN- number at post, replay, idempotent create, refusals (INVOICE_NOT_POSTED, date, LINE_NOT_ON_INVOICE, CREDIT_EXCEEDS_INVOICE incl. earlier credits), posted immutable.
- [x] **SS-PLAT-161** (db-integration, added) — ADR-0044 W6: invoice line postings recorded at invoice post; credit lines link to the invoice line and take its account and tax-code version (database-enforced, also after a rate change); `creditNoteAdjustmentLines` exposes the link.
- [x] **SS-PLAT-162** (db-integration, added) — credit open item allocated to the invoice; draft not allocatable; preview = posted journal, writes nothing.
- [x] **SS-PLAT-163** (db-integration, added) — always titled "Adjustment note" with original invoice number/date (GP-005, DL-0322); `later_bas_period` per company BAS frequency.
