## Report (claude, 2026-10-25, branch feat/T-0076-financial-statements)

**Built**
- `packages/ledger` read API `accountMovements({ companyId, from?, to, dimensionValueIds? })`
  (in `account-balance.ts`): posted base-currency debit/credit per account; whole periods from
  `account_balance`, cut periods from posted lines by `entry_date`; drafts and other companies
  left out; dimension filter = sets holding every requested value. Materialised CTEs keep the
  plan sane on freshly grown tables (DL-0173).
- New module `packages/modules/reporting` (`@ledgerforce/reporting`, design §8.1 *Reporting*):
  pure shaping in `statements.ts` (`shapeTrialBalance`, `shapeProfitAndLoss`,
  `shapeBalanceSheet`, `profitOf`) and db reports in `reports.ts`: `trialBalanceReport`,
  `profitAndLossReport`, `balanceSheetReport` (retained earnings = posted + prior-year P&L,
  current-year earnings line = P&L for the fiscal year to date, both `computed`, `balanced`),
  `trialBalanceReview` (SS-UC10-08), `ReportError` (`INVALID_RANGE`, `UNKNOWN_COMPANY`,
  `NO_FISCAL_YEAR`, `UNKNOWN_PERIOD`, `UNBOUND_ROLE`). Money is `Money`/decimal.js; no floats.
- No migration (0028 unused), no ADR (ADR-0033 unused), no third-party dependency.
- Research RQ-0037 (resolved); decisions DL-0172..0176.

**Tests** (all post raw journals through the engine; no event posting rule)
- `packages/ledger/src/account-movements.db.test.ts` (SS-PLAT-76, 4 tests).
- `packages/modules/reporting/src/statements.test.ts` (SS-PLAT-76 unit, SS-PLAT-77 fast-check
  pure property; 8 tests).
- `packages/modules/reporting/src/reports.tb-pl-bs.db.test.ts` (SS-UC10-08, SS-PLAT-76; 9 tests).
- `packages/modules/reporting/src/reports.prop.db.test.ts` (SS-PLAT-77, default numRuns, ~8 s):
  random ledgers over FY2026–FY2027 — TB nets to zero and equals a model, BS balances,
  CYE/prior-year earnings and P&L ranges equal the model.
- RED captured for the first three files against throwing stubs; the db property test was
  written after the implementation (its power shown by the mutation run).

**Mutation check** (10 hand mutants, all killed): drafts counted in cut periods; ANY instead of
ALL dimension filter; cut period without the date filter; CYE including prior years;
netsToZero always true; expenses credit-positive; RE line without prior profit; AR control
sign flipped; date round-trip check dropped; prior years ending on the FY start day. The first
run left "drafts counted" alive (drafts normally have no period); a draft carrying its October
period was added to the movements test, then it was killed.
