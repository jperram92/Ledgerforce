# T-0183 — Wizard step 8 and the remittance account screen (split from T-0180, DL-0634)

## Goal
T-0180 stores the company's remittance bank account by maker-checker (migration 0071, ADR-0079,
ADR-0080), prints it on invoices and offers the HTTP routes. This card finishes the user side:

- Wizard step 8 lists "no remittance account" when bank transfer is accepted and none is in force
  (ADR-0079 decision 2, T-0180 AC-3).
- A settings screen requests, approves and rejects a change. Account numbers are always masked.
  Users without `settings.setup.manage` see the screen read-only. The requester sees that a second
  person must approve. SoD and step-up refusals are explained in plain words.

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- AC-1 Step 8 lists "No remittance bank account has been chosen for bank transfer" when
  `bank_transfer` is accepted and no account is in force. It does not list it with Stripe only, or
  once an approved account is in force. Test: `packages/modules/setup/src/wizard-settings.db.test.ts`.
  Update the step-8 fixtures in the setup, wizard-flow and `apps/api/src/setup` tests that accept
  bank transfer: they approve a remittance account first, with no assertion weakened.
- AC-2 Screen: request a change, then a second user approves it. The step-up prompt appears; the
  requester is told beforehand they cannot approve; a rejection needs a reason; the view is
  masked. Test: e2e-playwright with screenshots at 1440 and 390, plus a register row.

## Open questions
- None. The routes and rules are in ADR-0080. Place the screen beside T-0176's step 8 settings
  screen.
