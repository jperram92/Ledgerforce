# T-0130 — Bank deposit of undeposited funds

## Goal
Receipts recorded before banking (cheque/cash) debit `UNDEPOSITED_FUNDS` (T-0046, GP-002
variant). Add the deposit that moves one or more such receipts to a company bank account in a
single entry (Dr bank / Cr UNDEPOSITED_FUNDS), so that the bank statement line of the deposit
matches one entry in reconciliation (UC-08).

## Context (design refs)
- Design §3.8 row 2 ("Bank or undeposited funds"); GP-002 *Variant* line; RQ-0112 follow-ups.
- Depends on: T-0046 (receipts, `app.ar_receipt.deposit_to = 'undeposited_funds'`).

## Open questions (→ research / golden before start)
- GP-002 states the variant only in words. A full golden table for the deposit entry (and its
  source type: none of `period-matrix.ts` SOURCE_TYPES is a bank deposit) must be proposed with
  skill `golden-posting` and **Approved** before any code — human gate (AGENTS.md §6.2).
- How a deposit records which receipts it banks (deposit slip document) — research, additive
  table with RLS + cross-tenant coverage.

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test + SS id)
- [ ] **SS-PLAT-155** (golden + db-integration) — Given two cash/cheque receipts on

  entry Dr bank / Cr UNDEPOSITED_FUNDS for their sum posts (equal to its Approved golden),
  each receipt is deposited at most once, and UNDEPOSITED_FUNDS nets to 0.00 for them.
