# T-0164 — Default payment terms set the due date of new invoices

## Goal
Follow-up from T-0158 (RQ-0153 conclusion 4, ADR-0071, DL-0555). `createInvoice` in
`packages/modules/ar/src/invoices.ts` falls back to the constant `DEFAULT_TERMS_DAYS = 14` when the
caller gives no due date. Use `app.ar_settings.default_payment_terms_days`, saved in wizard step 8,
instead. Keep 14 only when the company has saved no terms. Party-level terms (design §4.2) do not
exist yet. When a later card adds them, they win over the company default.

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- AC-1 Given terms of 30 days saved with `setInvoiceSettings`, when an invoice is created without
  a due date and issued 2026-11-13, then its due date is 2026-12-13 →
  `packages/modules/ar/src/payment-terms.db.test.ts`
- AC-2 Given no saved terms, the due date is the issue date + 14 days. An explicit due date always
  wins → same file
