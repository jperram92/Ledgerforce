# T-0136 — BAS 1B presentation of later-period credit notes

## Goal
T-0035's BAS report nets every credit note into G1/1A because the ledger lacked the credit note →
original invoice link (T-0035 card, open follow-up (1)). T-0045 stores it: each posted credit-note
line links to the invoice line it adjusts, and the credit note records `later_bas_period`
(company `bas_frequency`). `creditNoteAdjustmentLines(trx, { companyId, from, to })` in
`@ledgerforce/ar` returns, per posted credit-note line, the invoice, its date, entry and line, the
net and tax, and the flag. Use it in `basReport` (accruals): later-period credit notes → **1B +tax,
G1 unchanged** (default `adjustment_1B`); a company option `sales_adjustment_presentation =
adjustment_1B | net_G1_1A` keeps netting for accountants who prefer it (GP-005 Tax/BAS note). Cash
basis: per RQ-0102 (settlement attribution).
