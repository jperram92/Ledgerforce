# T-0201 — Opening balances: Xero reader

## Goal
Design §4.1 step 9 ("CSV or Xero import") and §9 Phase 1 ("Xero/MYOB importer"). Split from T-0126
(DL-0745). Read the old Xero organisation's trial balance and the open AR/AP documents at the balance
date into the same `OpeningBalanceCommand` the CSV path builds (T-0080, T-0126), so they post through
`postOpeningBalance` unchanged (GP-037, GP-040).

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- [ ] AC-1 A recorded Xero API fixture (`/Reports/TrialBalance?date=`, `/Invoices` ACCREC/ACCPAY
  AUTHORISED, credit notes, overpayments/prepayments) becomes the same command as the equivalent CSV
  → `packages/connectors/xero/src/opening.contract.test.ts`
- [ ] AC-2 Amounts are as at the balance date: payments and allocations dated after it are added back;
  documents dated after it are left out (RQ-0170 finding 7)
- [ ] AC-3 A multicurrency document becomes a foreign item with the rate converted from Xero's
  convention to base-per-foreign (RQ-0170 finding 4), verified on a recorded fixture

## Open questions
- Verify on developer.xero.com: rate limits, granular vs broad OAuth 2.0 scopes for new apps, and the
  `CurrencyRate` direction (RQ-0170 S7 was unreachable). Connection model and token storage need a
  security ADR. A new runtime dependency (e.g. Xero SDK) needs an ADR; prefer plain HTTPS.
