# T-0205 — Receipt limit uses the varied contract total (split from T-0203 AC-10, DL-0763)

## Goal

T-0203 refuses a single-supply receipt that would take receipts above the contract's known
total (`ABOVE_CONTRACT_TOTAL`). After T-0204 builds the contract-variation document (T-0204
AC-6, s 29-20(1)), the limit must use the total **as varied** at the receipt date. Otherwise an
agreed increase blocks lawful receipts and a decrease lets too much in (accounting review of
T-0168, finding 7).

## Acceptance criteria (failing tests to write first)

- AC-1 (`packages/modules/ar/src/single-supply.db.test.ts`): after an agreed increase recorded by
  the T-0204 variation document, a receipt up to the varied gross total is accepted. A receipt
  above it is refused with `ABOVE_CONTRACT_TOTAL`, and nothing is written.
- AC-2: after an agreed decrease, a receipt above the decreased total is refused. The receipt
  that reaches the varied total takes the remaining GST_ATTRIBUTED_UNBILLED balance as its GST
  part (T-0203 AC-8 rule on the varied GST).
- AC-3 (property, default numRuns): over random variations and receipts, the T-0203 invariants
  hold on the varied totals.
