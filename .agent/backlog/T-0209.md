# T-0209 — Failed security notices are visible and can be retried now

## Goal
T-0197 (ADR-0092, DL-0783) retries a failed security notice every 6 h at most, and never gives
up. The failure is recorded in `app.security_notice_delivery` and the notice stays `failed`, but
no screen shows it. The design rule (design §5.6, apps/worker AGENTS.md) is that failures that keep
going end up in front of a person. `app.sync_issue` is keyed to outbox events (migration 0030), so
it cannot hold these failures as it stands. Decide how to surface them: a Sync-issues inbox source,
or an admin alert. Then show failed notices to Owners and Finance Admins, with the masked
recipient, attempts and last error, and a retry-now action that makes the notice due at once
without editing history.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC-1 (db) a notice with N failed attempts is listed for Owners and Finance Admins of its tenant only, with attempts, last SMTP code and the next attempt time
- [ ] AC-2 (db) retry-now makes the notice due at the next dispatch (an appended row, nothing updated or deleted); the next dispatch sends it
- [ ] AC-3 (e2e) the list and the retry action on the admin screen, with screenshots
