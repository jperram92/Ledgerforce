# T-0232 — Contract-variation document dated at agreement

Split from T-0217 (DL-0900, RQ-0184 Q7). Accounting review of T-0168 finding 7: on the accruals
basis a price change is attributed to the period in which the supplier becomes aware of it
(s 29-20(1), ATO extract), so a variation agreed before the final invoice belongs to the period of
agreement, not the invoice period.

## Acceptance criteria (failing tests to write first)

- T-0204 AC-6 (rest, finding 7, s 29-20(1)): a contract-variation document dated at agreement
  posts the price delta lines (the GP-045b/c delta lines, without the AR/clearing lines) in the
  period of agreement; the final invoice then shows the varied price with no further GST. Needs
  candidate goldens (GP-047 onwards, next free ids) approved with research under DL-0263 before
  code. Until built, `finaliseSingleSupply` refuses VARIATION_NOT_ON_INVOICE_DATE (T-0204).
- A decrease carries adjustment-note particulars (same shape as 45c / 44a), also within $75.
- A variation on a cancelled or finalised contract is refused; a variation that would bring the
  varied total below the cash received is refused until a golden covers it.

## Open questions

- The journal of the variation itself: does the delta GST move through GST_ATTRIBUTED_UNBILLED
  (it is not yet received) and how is the varied total recorded for receipts (T-0205)? Research
  record (next free RQ id), comparable systems, then candidate goldens before any code.
- Primary law (s 29-20, Div 19) is unreadable from the build sandbox as of 2026-11-23
  (`.agent/evidence/T-0217/primary-source-probe.txt`); record, do not claim.
