# T-0197 — Security notice dispatcher

## Goal
The database writes durable security notices, but nothing sends them yet.
`app.bank_detail_change_notice` (migration 0025, T-0053) tells the previous remittance contact of
a supplier bank-detail change. `app.remittance_account_notice` (migration 0072, T-0191, ADR-0086)
tells every Owner and Finance Admin of an approved invoice remittance account change. Build one
dispatcher in the worker that sends `pending` and `failed` notices through the email module and
records delivery. lf_app may only move a notice `pending/failed → sent/failed`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC-1 (db) a pending remittance account notice is sent once to its recipient, with masked details only (no clear number), and becomes `sent` with `sent_at`; a redelivery does not send it again
- [ ] AC-2 (db) a pending bank-detail change notice is sent the same way; `no_recipient` rows are never sent
- [ ] AC-3 (db) an SMTP failure marks the notice `failed` and it is retried with backoff; another tenant's notices are never read or sent
