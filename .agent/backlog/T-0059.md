## Goal
Extend the CAMT reader in `@ledgerforce/bank` with: (1) batch entries split into their `NtryDtls/TxDtls`
transactions (today a batch stays one line with the booked amount); (2) several `Stmt` per camt.053 file
(refused today with a clear message), each imported against its own bank account; (3) camt.052 (intraday report)
and camt.054 (debit/credit notification) where an AU bank offers them — research which do.
