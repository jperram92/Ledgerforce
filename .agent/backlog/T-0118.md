# T-0118 — Tax codes in the database: effective-dated tax_code table, company rounding setting, line wiring

Persist the AU tax-code catalogue (packages/modules/tax AU_TAX_CODES) per company with effective dating, the company `tax_rounding` setting (total_invoice default, per_line_2dp opt-in), and make journal_line.tax_code_id reference it.
