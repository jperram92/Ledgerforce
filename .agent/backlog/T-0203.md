# T-0203 — Single-supply receipt posting (ADR-0089 R1–R5, R8, R9, R12)

## Goal

Build the production path for GP-040a/b on the accruals basis and the GP-020 path for a single
supply on the cash basis, under Accepted ADR-0089. No accounting decision is open: goldens
GP-040…GP-042 are Approved (DL-0751…DL-0753).

## Acceptance criteria (failing tests to write first)

- AC-1 (migration, next free number after round 9 is merged — 0077 was reserved but leaves a gap on
  the T-0168 branch, DL-0754): `GST_ATTRIBUTED_UNBILLED` extended role (widen
  `app.system_role_is_extended` and `app.system_role_allows`, asset, not control, ADR-0061 pattern);
  `app.single_supply_contract` (tenant_id, company_id, party_id, known_net NUMERIC(19,4),
  tax_code_id, attributed_tax, attribution_date, gst_basis, status, confirmed_by/at, idempotency
  key; RLS ENABLE + FORCE; lf_app SELECT/INSERT only; immutable rows, status by successor rows);
  single-supply receipts are stored without widening the 0042 `supply_type` CHECK (that needs a
  DROP, forbidden): a separate immutable `app.single_supply_receipt` table (contract FK, gross, net,
  tax, entry, idempotency key) — the db-engineer may choose another additive shape and record it.
  Tests: `packages/db/src/migrations.db.test.ts` (role allowed for asset only), cross-tenant suite
  rows for the new table, forced RLS as lf_app.
- AC-2: AU services template v-next binds account 1230 "GST attributed on unbilled supplies";
  `AU_SERVICES_BINDINGS['system:GST_ATTRIBUTED_UNBILLED']` → role; the golden engine replay then
  replays GP-040a/b, GP-041a–c, GP-042a and `PENDING` in `golden-engine.db.test.ts` returns to `{}`.
- AC-3 (`packages/modules/ar/src/single-supply.db.test.ts`): `recordSingleSupplyReceipt` posts
  GP-040a line for line on accruals (first receipt) and GP-040b (later receipts); on the cash basis
  in force at the receipt date it posts GP-020; refuses with nothing written: no contract total,
  mixed/second tax code, receipts above the total, a basis different from the contract's,
  `single_supply` deposit invoice on accruals (advice GP-021).
- AC-4: idempotency (same key posts once, returns the same view) and concurrency (two receipts in
  parallel on one contract: exactly one becomes "first", total GST attributed once — advisory lock
  per contract, as `applyPrepayment`).
- AC-5 (`*.prop.test.ts`, default numRuns): for random known totals and receipt sequences, every
  entry balances, Σ GST reported = GST on the known total, unbilled balance = attributed GST − GST
  received, and contract liability = net received.
- AC-6: the existing `single` refusal test in `prepayments.db.test.ts` stays until this card
  replaces it with the new path (R12); no test weakened.
