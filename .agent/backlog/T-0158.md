# T-0158 — Module-owned settings for wizard steps 3, 8 and 10

## Goal
Split from T-0123 (DL-0523). RQ-0149 Q2 found settings the wizard steps ask for that no module
persists yet. Add them to their owning modules (never the wizard) and extend the T-0123
validators so the steps require them:
- step 3 → **tax** module: GST accounting basis (cash | accrual) and BAS reporting frequency
  (monthly | quarterly | annual) per company, effective-dated;
- step 8 → **ar** module: invoice branding (logo document, colours), default payment terms and
  accepted payment methods (bank transfer, Stripe) per company;
- step 10 → **SoD settings** (auth): only the approval thresholds design §6.5 names that
  `app.sod_settings` (0021) lacks, if any (research first).

## Context (design refs)
- Design §3.6, §4.1 steps 3/8/10, §6.5; RQ-0149; ADR-0067.

  → `packages/modules/setup/src/wizard-settings.db.test.ts` (SS-PLAT-121)
