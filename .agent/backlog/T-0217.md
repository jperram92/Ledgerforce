# T-0217 — Single-supply cancellation, refund, reversal and variation document

Split from T-0204 (DL-0833, RQ-0178 Q5). T-0204 slice 1 built the accruals final invoice
(`packages/modules/ar/src/single-supply-final.ts`, migration 0078). The criteria below are the
T-0204 criteria moved here with their full text; their ids keep the T-0204 numbering.

## Acceptance criteria (failing tests to write first)

- T-0204 AC-2: cancellation with the refund agreed posts GP-044a (whole 1,000.00 decrease, one
  adjustment note, 3,300.00 customer credit) and the refund GP-044b through the refund approval
  path (ADR-0074, SoD) allocated to that credit; cancellation with the refund undecided posts
  GP-044c, and the refund once agreed GP-044d with its own adjustment note; forfeiture refused
  (named error). Contract status becomes cancelled.
- T-0204 AC-3: reversal of a GP-040a receipt before finalisation restores every balance
  (INV-reversal); reversal after finalisation is refused (credit note path instead, Q11).
- T-0204 AC-9 (MINOR-4): the reversal of a GP-040a receipt in a later period is recorded so BAS
  preparation (T-0218) labels it an error correction, not a Div 19 adjustment.
- T-0204 AC-10 (rest): adjustment-note particulars on the GP-044a and GP-044d documents; the $75
  threshold (s 29-80) only relaxes the obligation, the document still carries them.
- T-0204 AC-6 (rest, finding 7, s 29-20(1)): a contract-variation document dated at agreement
  posts the price delta lines (the GP-045b/c delta lines, without the AR/clearing lines) in the
  period of agreement; the final invoice then shows the varied price with no further GST. Needs
  candidate goldens (GP-047 onwards, next free ids) approved with research under DL-0263 before
  code. Until built, `finaliseSingleSupply` refuses VARIATION_NOT_ON_INVOICE_DATE (T-0204).
- Cash-basis final invoice (ADR-0089 R8): the final invoice of a cash-basis contract posts the
  ordinary invoice and applies the GP-020 receipts by GP-022. Until built it is refused
  CASH_BASIS_NOT_BUILT (T-0204). Also: a final invoice on an accruals contract with no receipt
  (NOTHING_ATTRIBUTED) — decide with research whether it is an ordinary invoice that finalises
  the contract.
- Final invoice delivery: the slice-1 final invoice publishes no `ar.invoice.posted` event (no
  e-mail); add it with the Billing To recipients as `approveInvoice` does.
