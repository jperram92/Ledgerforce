## Report (claude, parallel round 6, branch feat/T-0064-cdc-subscriber)

**Built**
- Research: RQ-0036 (resolved) covers the vendored proto, flow control (no more than 100
  events outstanding), replay ids and presets, the 72 h retention, the `error-code` trailer,
  Avro via GetSchema, ChangeEventHeader, and gap and overflow events. Sources are listed;
  developer.salesforce.com was reachable only through search extracts.
- ADR-0032 (Proposed, integration, no human gate) adds runtime dependencies pinned exact:
  `@grpc/grpc-js` 1.14.6, `@grpc/proto-loader` 0.8.1 and `avsc` 5.7.9. Each release is at least
  two weeks old, and `pnpm audit --prod` is clean.
- Migration 0026:
  - `app.salesforce_replay_cursor`, one row per company and channel, moved by compare-and-set;
  - `app.salesforce_event_inbox`, append-only idempotency keys;
  - `app.salesforce_resync_request`, holding gap, overflow and replay_id_invalid rows, where
    only `resolved_at` may change.

  All three have RLS ENABLE and FORCE, a tenant_isolation policy and explicit lf_app grants,
  and are covered by the cross-tenant suite (new overrides were added).
- `packages/connectors/salesforce/src/pubsub/` contains:
  - `proto.ts`, which loads the vendored proto (SHA-256 pinned);
  - `avro.ts`, with BigInt longs, field bitmaps, change kinds and idempotency keys;
  - `errors.ts`, which classifies errors by the `error-code` trailer;
  - `client.ts`, the gRPC client with TLS and call-metadata auth (plaintext to loopback only);
  - `replay-store.ts`;
  - `subscriber.ts` (`runSubscription`). Each batch runs in one tenant transaction (cursor
    compare-and-set, inbox, handler, resync rows). More events are requested only after the
    commit. A keepalive advances the cursor. A rejected replay id leads to a cursor reset, a
    resync request "since the last commit", and the next run starting at LATEST.
- Test-only pieces: a local fake Pub/Sub gRPC server built from the same proto
  (`src/testing/fake-pubsub-server.ts`), an Avro encoder (`src/testing/cdc-events.ts`), and
  documented fixtures with their generator script.
- Decision log: DL-0167 to DL-0171.

**Tests**
- `pubsub.decode.contract.test.ts` (SS-PLAT-75): 12 tests.
- `replay-resume.db.test.ts` (SS-PLAT-27 and SS-PLAT-74): 10 tests. They cover:
  - a crash mid-batch, then a restart from R with nothing lost or doubled;
  - a redelivered event being dropped;
  - flow control;
  - the keepalive cursor;
  - a 250-event outage replayed in batches of 100;
  - a compare-and-set conflict;
  - an expired replay id causing a reset and resync;
  - gap and overflow events becoming resync requests;
  - tenant isolation;
  - grants and the append-only inbox.
- RED was captured first: both files failed with missing modules. Outputs are in the scratchpad.
