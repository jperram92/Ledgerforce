## Report (claude, 2026-10-26)
**Built.**
- API (ADR-0042, DL-0225), test-first:
  - `GET /accounts` (gl.account.read): companies with ids, and the chart with system roles.
  - `GET /manual-journals` (gl.journal.read): newest 200, `?status=`, amounts summed by PostgreSQL, names.
  - `GET /manual-journals/:id/preview` (gl.journal.read): the engine's `preview()` of the stored
    document's command, with account names; 422 CONTROL_ACCOUNT with its line; ALREADY_POSTED with
    `entry_no`.
  - `people` (id → name) on `GET /manual-journals/:id`.
  - The reads live in packages/modules/gl and packages/modules/journals. nginx proxies `accounts`.
- Web (DL-0226, DL-0227):
  - `/accounting/chart`: by type with glosses; control, not postable and roles in words.
  - `/accounting/journals`: list with a status filter in the URL; New for preparers only.
  - `/accounting/journals/new` and `/accounting/journals/$id/edit`: the draft form, keyboard path,
    problems listed before sending, one Idempotency-Key per form.
  - `/accounting/journals/$id`: the engine preview in JournalTable with Balanced; the refused line
    marked "Refused" with the reason in plain words and Submit withheld; people involved; whose
    turn; submit, approve, send back and post by permission.
  - All screens are listed on the Reports page. The six-link navigation is unchanged.
  - Every screen has loading, empty, error (Try again) and forbidden states; axe 0 violations at
    1440 and 390.
- No migration (0031 unused). No new third-party dependency. `@ledgerforce/gl` moved from dev to
  runtime workspace dependency of apps/api.
