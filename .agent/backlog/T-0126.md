# T-0126 — Opening balances: foreign-currency open items and Xero import

## Goal
Design §4.1 step 9 says "CSV or Xero import". T-0080 imports the trial balance and base-currency
open items from CSV. This card adds (a) open items in a foreign currency (transaction amount and
rate, base amount summing to the trial-balance control amount; FX revaluation researched first) and
(b) reading the trial balance and open invoices/bills from Xero at the conversion date into the same
`postOpeningBalances` command.

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- [ ] AC-1 A USD open invoice posts with its transaction amount, rate and base amount and ages in
  base currency → `packages/modules/setup/src/opening-balances.fx.db.test.ts`
- [ ] AC-2 A recorded Xero API fixture (trial balance, invoices, bills) becomes the same command as
  the equivalent CSV → `packages/connectors/xero/src/opening.contract.test.ts`
