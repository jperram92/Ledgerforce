# T-0163 — UI: record the bank's confirmation of a payment batch, with the GP-009 preview

## Goal
Split from T-0154 (DL-0540): UC-06 step 4 on the payment batch screen. After the bank has paid,
a releaser enters the confirmation date and the bank's reference, sees the journal that will
post (GP-009, from the T-0162 preview route, open by default with a Balanced indicator — P4),
then records the confirmation (`POST /ap/payment-batches/:id/confirm`, T-0057). The screen then
shows the posted entry and the suppliers without a Remittance contact (`remittance_missing`), so
someone can add one. Nothing is computed in the browser; no unmasked account number shows.

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- AC-1 Given a released batch, when a releaser enters the date and bank reference, then the
  GP-009 preview from the API shows its lines and Balanced before anything posts →
  `tests/e2e/web/payment-batch.spec.ts`
- AC-2 When the releaser records the confirmation, then the screen shows the posted entry and
  the suppliers in `remittance_missing`; a repeat shows the first confirmation →
  `tests/e2e/web/payment-batch.spec.ts`
- AC-3 Someone without `ap.payment_batch.release` sees no confirmation action; refusals
  (422 NOT_CONFIRMABLE, INVALID_BATCH, 503 queue_not_configured, any step-up or SoD answer) in
  plain words → `tests/e2e/web/payment-batch.spec.ts`
