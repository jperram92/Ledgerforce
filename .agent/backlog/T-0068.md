# T-0068 — Basic overdue reminders (UC-04 MVP scope)

## Goal
Daily schedule sends reminder 1 at 7 days overdue to Billing To, sets LF_Credit_Status__c "Overdue"; idempotent per invoice/step.

## Context (design refs)
- Design: UC-04 (credit hold + Tasks are Phase 1).
- Specs: [UC-04](../../Docs/specs/UC-04.md) · Register: [success-scenarios.md](../../Docs/specs/success-scenarios.md)
- Depends on: T-0049 (Customer statements and aged receivables); T-0042 (Email delivery (SMTP) via outbox for invoices, statements, reminders, remittances); T-0067 (Write-back to Salesforce via sObject Collections from the outbox)

## Open questions (→ research before start)
- None known. Re-read design refs at claim; open an RQ for anything unclear.

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test + SS id)
- [ ] **SS-UC04-01** (db-integration) — Reminder 1 at 7 days overdue. *Given* an invoice 7 days overdue; *when* the reminder schedule runs; *then* the Billing To contact receives reminder 1 (SMTP sink).
- [ ] **SS-UC04-02** (contract) — Credit status "Overdue" written back. *Given* SS-UC04-01 has run; *when* write-back runs; *then* the payload sets Account.LF_Credit_Status__c = "Overdue".
- [ ] **SS-UC04-03** (db-integration) — Reminders are idempotent per step. *Given* SS-UC04-01 has run; *when* the schedule runs again the same day; *then* no second reminder is sent.
- [ ] **SS-UC04-04** (unit) — Paid invoice gets no reminder. *Given* an invoice paid on day 6; *when* the schedule runs on day 7; *then* no reminder is queued.
