# T-0109 — verify gate 9 writes evidence only for the task being verified

## Goal
`bash scripts/agent/verify.sh --task T-X` runs `pnpm test:e2e`, and the Playwright evidence fixture
writes screenshots and manifest entries for every spec's own task (T-0009, T-0106 …), not for T-X.
Every verify run therefore adds about 30 PNGs to `.agent/evidence/T-0009/`. They were committed in
the "verified → review" commits from T-0017 to T-0011 (DL-0098). They are genuine re-runs, so they
are kept (directive 6), but they are noise filed under the wrong task.
