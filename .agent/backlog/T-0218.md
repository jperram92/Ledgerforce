# T-0218 — Single-supply BAS preparation and the primary-law reading

Split from T-0204 (DL-0833, RQ-0178 Q5). The criteria keep their T-0204 numbering and full text.
`adr_required`: the `credit_note_bas` company setting changes BAS presentation (accounting ADR,
GP-029 already describes both presentations).

## Acceptance criteria (failing tests to write first)

- T-0204 AC-4 (`packages/modules/tax/src/bas.db.test.ts` or a new BAS-prep test): the BAS query
  refuses or flags a basis different from the basis in force for the period (RQ-0171); the
  BAS-preparation data lists each contributing prepayment's supply pattern and every contract
  with a non-zero GST_ATTRIBUTED_UNBILLED balance older than 90 days (aged unbilled report),
  cross-tenant safe.
- T-0204 AC-7 (finding 11): BAS preparation never presents a negative G1 or 1A: a netted label
  below zero is presented as a decreasing adjustment (G18 worksheet / 1B), or the company is
  required to use `credit_note_bas = ato_adjustment`; golden + test (e.g. GP-044a alone in a
  period). Confirm against the current ATO BAS instructions (primary text).
- `credit_note_bas` company setting (GP-029; it does not exist in the schema): default `net`;
  `ato_adjustment` makes the GST line of a credit note, of a GP-045c/GP-044 decrease and of the
  T-0204 final invoice price reduction carry GST-ADJ (T-0204 AC-1 last sentence, moved here).
- T-0204 AC-5 (rest, `*.prop.test.ts`, default numRuns): over random lifecycles including
  cancellation and refund, GST reported per contract equals GST on the final consideration and
  the unbilled balance ends at zero. (Receipts + final invoice are covered by SS-PLAT-159.)
- T-0204 AC-9 presentation: a later-period reversal of a GP-040a receipt is labelled an error
  correction in BAS preparation.
- T-0204 AC-11 (RQ-0171 addendum A, RQ-0178 Q6): before this card closes, read the primary text
  of GST Act s 29-5, 29-20, 29-70, 29-75, 29-80, 19-10, 19-45, GSTR 2000/29, GSTR 2001/8, the
  current ATO BAS instructions, AASB Interpretation 1031 and AASB 101 para 32 and record the
  result in an RQ; any difference supersedes the goldens. Unreachable from the build environment
  on 2026-11-16 and 2026-11-22 (`.agent/evidence/T-0204/ac11-primary-source-probe.txt`).
