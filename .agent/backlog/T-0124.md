# T-0124 — Receipts, refunds and payment batches settle opening AR/AP items

## Goal
Opening open items (GP-037, T-0080) are paid like invoices and bills: `recordReceipt` /
`allocateReceipt` accept an opening AR item as an allocation target (today they accept only
`app.sales_invoice` ids), and payment batches include due opening AP items next to bills (one GP-009
AP line per item). Ageing and the ledger's `allocate()` already work on them (T-0080, SS-PLAT-115).

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- [ ] AC-1 A receipt allocated to an opening AR item closes it (GP-002 journal unchanged) →
  `packages/modules/ar/src/receipts.opening.db.test.ts`
- [ ] AC-2 A payment batch pays an opening AP item; confirmation posts GP-009 with one AP line for it
  → `packages/modules/ap/src/payment-batch.opening.db.test.ts`
