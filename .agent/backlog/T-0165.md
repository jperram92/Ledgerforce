# T-0165 — Invoice document shows branding and payment methods

## Goal
Follow-up from T-0158 (RQ-0153 conclusion 4, ADR-0071, DL-0555). The invoice document
(`packages/modules/documents/src/invoice-document.ts`, `invoice-html.ts`, PDF via T-0041) should
show the brand colour, the optional logo, and how to pay. That is bank transfer (remittance
details of the company bank account) and/or a Stripe payment link, depending on
`app.ar_settings.accepted_payment_methods` (design §4.1 step 8).

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- AC-1 Given a brand colour and a PNG logo, the rendered invoice HTML uses the colour and embeds
  the logo as a data URI. Without a logo, no image element is rendered →
  `packages/modules/documents/src/invoice-html.test.ts`
- AC-2 Given accepted methods `[bank_transfer]`, the HTML shows the bank-transfer block and no
  Stripe link. With `[stripe]` it shows the Stripe block. With both it shows both → same file
- AC-3 The PDF of a branded invoice renders (page count, text present) →
  `packages/modules/documents/src/invoice-pdf.db.test.ts`

## Open questions
- Which bank account's remittance details are printed when the company has several? This needs
  research before step 2 (it may need a "remittance account" setting, which would be a data
  change and an ADR).
- The Stripe payment link source (connector-stripe) and its phase.
