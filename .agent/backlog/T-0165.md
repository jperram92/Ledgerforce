# T-0165 — Sales-document rounding presented as a revenue adjustment (optional report setting)

## Goal
The accountant-reviewer persona (DL-0280, MINOR "GP-018b (revenue)") noted that under `per_document`
GST rounding the ex-GST transaction price is 9.14 (AASB 15 ¶47) while the golden posts revenue 9.15
and a 0.01 ROUNDING expense (chosen treatment, DL-0244/DL-0255). T-0124 recorded this as the Draft
note **E-004** in `Docs/accounting/postings.md`. This card adds the optional **report** rule: the
profit and loss statement may present ROUNDING lines that come from sales documents (`ar.invoice`,
`ar.credit_note`) as a revenue adjustment (net revenue), behind a company report setting that is off
by default. No posting changes; the ledger keeps the ROUNDING line.

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- [ ] **AC-1** With the setting off (default) the P&L is unchanged: ROUNDING stays an expense line.
  → `packages/modules/reports/src/pnl-rounding.db.test.ts` (path to be confirmed against T-0076)
- [ ] **AC-2** With the setting on, GP-018b's 0.01 ROUNDING from INV-000160 is shown inside revenue
  (net revenue 9.14); ROUNDING from journals or AP documents stays an expense. Net profit is the same
  in both modes. → same file
