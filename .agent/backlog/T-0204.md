# T-0204 — Single-supply finalisation, cancellation and reports (ADR-0089 R6, R7, R10, R11)

## Acceptance criteria (failing tests to write first)

- AC-1 (`packages/modules/ar/src/single-supply-final.db.test.ts`): finalising a contract issues the
  tax invoice showing the fixed GST (GP-023a residual distribution over lines) and posts GP-045a
  (whole tax invoice on AR, the cash received as an AR credit allocated to it at once); a price
  increase agreed on the invoice date posts GP-045b, a decrease GP-045c with adjustment-note
  particulars; with
  `credit_note_bas = ato_adjustment` the GST line carries GST-ADJ (as GP-029). Contract status
  becomes finalised; a second finalisation is refused (idempotent replay returns the same invoice).
- AC-2: cancellation with the refund agreed posts GP-044a (whole 1,000.00 decrease, one adjustment
  note, 3,300.00 customer credit) and the refund GP-044b through the refund approval path
  (ADR-0074, SoD) allocated to that credit; cancellation with the refund undecided posts GP-044c,
  and the refund once agreed GP-044d with its own adjustment note; forfeiture refused (named
  error).
- AC-3: reversal of a GP-040a receipt before finalisation restores every balance (INV-reversal);
  reversal after finalisation is refused (credit note path instead, Q11).
- AC-4 (`packages/modules/tax/src/bas.db.test.ts` or a new BAS-prep test): the BAS query refuses or
  flags a basis different from the basis in force for the period (RQ-0171); the BAS-preparation
  data lists each contributing prepayment's supply pattern and every contract with a non-zero
  GST_ATTRIBUTED_UNBILLED balance older than 90 days (aged unbilled report), cross-tenant safe.
- AC-5 (`*.prop.test.ts`, default numRuns): over random lifecycles (receipts, price change,
  cancellation, refund), GST reported per contract equals GST on the final consideration and the
  unbilled balance ends at zero.

## Acceptance criteria added by the T-0168 accounting review (DL-0757)

- AC-6 (finding 7, s 29-20(1)): a contract-variation document dated at agreement posts the price
  delta lines (the GP-045b/c delta lines, without the AR/clearing lines) in the period of
  agreement; the final invoice then shows the varied price with no further GST. Until it is
  built a final price different from the contract is refused unless the variation date equals
  the invoice date. Candidate goldens (next free GP ids) approved with research before code.
- AC-7 (finding 11): BAS preparation never presents a negative G1 or 1A: a netted label below
  zero is presented as a decreasing adjustment (G18 worksheet / 1B), or the company is required
  to use `credit_note_bas = ato_adjustment`; golden + test (e.g. GP-044a alone in a period).
  Confirm against the current ATO BAS instructions (primary text).
- AC-8 (finding 15): a final price below the cash already received is refused with a named
  error until a golden for the credit/refund path exists.
- AC-9 (MINOR-4): BAS preparation labels a reversal of a GP-040a receipt in a later period as an
  error correction, not a Div 19 adjustment.
- AC-10 (findings 8/10): adjustment-note particulars on GP-044a, GP-044d and GP-045c documents;
  the $75 threshold (s 29-80) only relaxes the obligation, the document still carries them.
- AC-11 (RQ-0171 addendum A): before this card closes, read the primary text of GST Act s 29-5,
  29-20, 29-70, 29-75, 29-80, 19-10, 19-45, GSTR 2000/29, GSTR 2001/8, the current ATO BAS
  instructions, AASB Interpretation 1031 and AASB 101 para 32 (legislation.gov.au, ato.gov.au
  and aasb.gov.au were unreachable from the build environment on 2026-11-16) and record the
  result in an RQ; any difference supersedes the goldens.
