# T-0040 — Sales invoice lifecycle: draft → approve → post

## Goal
Invoice document, approval (Billing To required), posting via engine producing GP-001; credit-hold check hook; auto-approve threshold setting.

## Context (design refs)
- Design: §3.8 row 1, §4.2 rule, UC-01.
- Specs: [UC-01](../../Docs/specs/UC-01.md) · Register: [success-scenarios.md](../../Docs/specs/success-scenarios.md)
- Depends on: T-0039 (Posting-profile matrix resolution and gap check); T-0025 (Posting engine pipeline (command → validate → resolve → build → check → write)); T-0034 (Tax codes (effective-dated) and GST engine with rounding); T-0028 (Subledger entries and allocations); T-0015 (Segregation-of-duties engine and small-team mode); T-0026 (Posting engine preview mode ("show me the journal")); T-0031 (Golden-posting test runner)

## Open questions (→ research before start)

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test + SS id)
- [ ] **SS-UC01-03** (golden) — Auto-approve under threshold posts GP-001 and emails. *Given* auto-approve on with threshold $2,000 and the SS-UC01-01 Opportunity; *when* the draft is created; *then* the invoice is posted with exactly the GP-001 journal, numbered INV-…, and one email is sent to the Billing To contact.
- [ ] **SS-UC01-05** (db-integration) — No Billing To contact blocks approval. *Given* an Account without a Billing To contact; *when* approval of its draft is attempted; *then* approval is rejected with reason NO_BILLING_TO and nothing posts.
- [ ] **SS-UC01-11** (golden) — Tax-inclusive rounding follows company setting. *Given* three $3.35 GST-inclusive lines; *when* posted under per_line and per_document settings; *then* journals equal GP-018a and GP-018b respectively.
