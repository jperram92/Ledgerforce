## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test + SS id)
- [x] **SS-UC01-03** (golden) — Auto-approve under threshold posts GP-001 and emails. *Given* auto-approve on with threshold $2,000 and the SS-UC01-01 Opportunity; *when* the draft is created; *then* the invoice is posted with exactly the GP-001 journal, numbered INV-…, and one email is sent to the Billing To contact.
- [x] **SS-UC01-05** (db-integration) — No Billing To contact blocks approval. *Given* an Account without a Billing To contact; *when* approval of its draft is attempted; *then* approval is rejected with reason NO_BILLING_TO and nothing posts.
- [x] **SS-UC01-11** (golden) — Tax-inclusive rounding follows company setting. *Given* three $3.35 GST-inclusive lines; *when* posted under per_line and per_document settings; *then* journals equal GP-018a and GP-018b respectively.
- [x] **SS-PLAT-120** (db-integration, added) — draft → approved → posted through the engine; INV- number taken at post (consecutive); AR open item with due date; replay; posted invoice and lines immutable; idempotent create; auto-approve off/above threshold/credit hold stays draft; settings history.
- [x] **SS-PLAT-121** (db-integration, added) — invoice preview equals the posted journal and writes nothing.
- [x] **SS-PLAT-122** (db-integration, added) — approval refuses TAX_CODE_CONFLICT (explicit line tax code needed, DL-0260) and NO_PARTY_PROFILE; nothing posts.
