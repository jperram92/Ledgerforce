# T-0180 — Remittance bank account on invoices

## Goal
Follow-up from T-0165 (RQ-0159 conclusion 2, ADR-0079). When a company accepts bank transfer
(design §4.1 step 8), its invoices show the account name, BSB and account number of **one chosen**
company bank account, the remittance account. T-0165 already renders that block when the document
carries `bankTransfer`. This card stores the choice and fills it.

## Blocked
- ADR-0079 is a **security** ADR (a second permitted use of the decrypting call of ADR-0018) and
  stays Proposed until the product owner accepts it.
- It needs a migration (nullable `app.ar_settings.remittance_bank_account_id`, composite FK to
  `app.bank_account`). Under DL-0564 sub-agents add no migration; the lead adds it.

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- AC-1 `setInvoiceSettings` stores a remittance account of the same company and refuses an
  archived account or one of another company → `packages/modules/ar/src/invoice-settings.db.test.ts`
- AC-2 With bank transfer accepted and a remittance account set, `loadInvoiceDocument` fills
  `bankTransfer` with the decrypted number of that account only; read APIs stay masked →
  `packages/modules/documents/src/invoice-pdf.db.test.ts`
- AC-3 Wizard step 8 lists "no remittance account" when bank transfer is accepted and none is set
  → `packages/modules/setup/src/wizard-settings.db.test.ts`
- AC-4 Changing the remittance account goes through the SoD bank-detail rule (requester ≠
  verifier, or a small-team exception) → db test

## Open questions
- None beyond the owner's decision on ADR-0079 (RQ-0159).
