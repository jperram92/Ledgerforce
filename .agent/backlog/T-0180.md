# T-0180 — Remittance bank account on invoices

## Goal
Follow-up from T-0165 (RQ-0159 conclusion 2, ADR-0079). When a company accepts bank transfer
(design §4.1 step 8), its invoices show the account name, BSB and account number of **one chosen**
company bank account, the remittance account. T-0165 already renders that block when the document
carries `bankTransfer`. This card stores the choice and fills it.

## Blocked (resolved)
- ADR-0079 was accepted on 2026-11-15 (DL-0568). Under DL-0569 this card owns migration 0071.

## Acceptance criteria (each maps to a test)
- AC-1 A remittance account of the same company is stored; an archived account or one of another
  company is refused → `packages/modules/ar/src/remittance-account.db.test.ts`. Accepting
  ADR-0079 made the setting maker-checker, so it is stored by an approved change, not by
  `setInvoiceSettings` (DL-0631).
- AC-2 With bank transfer accepted and a remittance account set, `loadInvoiceDocument` fills
  `bankTransfer` with the decrypted number of that account only; read APIs stay masked →
  `packages/modules/documents/src/invoice-pdf.db.test.ts`
- AC-3 Wizard step 8 lists "no remittance account" when bank transfer is accepted and none is set.
  **Moved to T-0183** with the settings screen (DL-0634, card split).
- AC-4 Changing the remittance account is maker-checker: requester ≠ approver, or a recorded
  small-team exception (SOD_REMITTANCE_SELF_APPROVAL) → `packages/modules/ar/src/remittance-account.db.test.ts`;
  over HTTP with settings.setup.manage, step-up and masked audit →
  `apps/api/src/setup/remittance-account.db.test.ts` (SS-UC01-17)
