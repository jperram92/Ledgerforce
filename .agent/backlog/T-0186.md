# T-0186 — Numbering and billing, and approval amounts (wizard steps 8 and 10)

## Goal
Split from T-0176 (DL-0640). Step 8: an Owner or Finance Admin sets up the invoice number series
(AR-INV), the standard billing formats, the brand colour, default payment terms and accepted
payment methods (`setInvoiceSettings`), and the Stripe clearing account when Stripe is accepted.
Step 10: the Owner enters the approval amounts (SoD thresholds) through the existing
`GET/POST /sod/settings` (settings.sod.manage, Owner only, with a reason). Both screens are linked
from the wizard's "where" line and their missing lists empty after saving.

## Context (design refs)
- Design §4.1 steps 8 and 10, §6.5; RQ-0153; RQ-0161; ADR-0071; ADR-0081 (route family).
- `packages/modules/ar/src/invoice-settings.ts`, `packages/modules/documents/src/billing-formats.ts`,
  `app.number_series`, `app.stripe_settings`, `apps/api/src/sod/sod.controller.ts`.
- `/sod/settings` POST takes no Idempotency-Key today (it is state-idempotent: the same values
  answer 200). Decide whether the screen sends one anyway and whether the route should require it.
