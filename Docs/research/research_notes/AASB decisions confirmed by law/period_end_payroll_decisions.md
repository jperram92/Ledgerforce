## D1. Payday Super: super payable due date per pay run, super-payment posting, overdue alerts, SG amount from payroll provider

### Takeaway
**CONFIRMED, with one wording change.** The Treasury Laws Amendment (Payday Superannuation) Act 2025 (Act No. 57 of 2025) received Royal Assent on **6 November 2025**. A companion SGC Amendment Act (No. 58 of 2025) received assent the same day. The new rules apply to qualifying earnings (QE) days on or after **1 July 2026**. Super must be **received by the fund** within 7 business days after payday. An extended period of **20 business days** applies to a new employee's first payday and to the next payday after an employee chooses a new fund. The SG rate stays at 12%. The ATO's Small Business Superannuation Clearing House (SBSCH) closed permanently on 1 July 2026. Xero, MYOB and Employment Hero (KeyPay) now calculate and pay super through their own clearing services on every pay run. The proposed design is therefore right. One refinement: the due date is a *receipt-by-fund* deadline, so the ledger should date the alert against that deadline and allow for clearing-house transit time.

### Cited Findings
- Act: Treasury Laws Amendment (Payday Superannuation) Act 2025, Act No. 57 of 2025, Royal Assent 6 Nov 2025. Superannuation Guarantee Charge Amendment Act 2025, Act No. 58 of 2025, same date. Parliament finished with the bills on 4 Nov 2025. — [Federal Register of Legislation C2025A00057](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025A00057) (via search text; fetch blocked); [Mondaq: Payday Super bills received Royal Assent, start date remains 1 July 2026](https://www.mondaq.com/australia/employee-rights-labour-relations/1707426/payday-super-bills-received-royal-assent-and-start-date-remains-1-july-2026); [APH bill r7373](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Bills_Legislation/Bills_Search_Results/Result?bId=r7373); [APH SGC Amendment Bill r7374](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Bills_Legislation/Bills_Search_Results/Result?bId=r7374). (First-round note: Lawpath/Capricorn said "passed 6 Nov 2025". That date is in fact the assent date; passage was 4 Nov.)
- Commencement: the laws apply to SG contributions for qualifying earnings days on or after 1 July 2026 — [Andersen November 2025 Monthly Tax Update](https://au.andersen.com/november-2025-monthly-tax-update/); [Super Members Council / ASFA overview](https://www.superannuation.asn.au/payday-super-for-super-funds-overview).
- Treasury Laws Amendment (Payday Superannuation) Regulations 2026, made 19 Feb 2026 — [ASFA summary](https://www.superannuation.asn.au/payday-super-for-super-funds-overview) (secondary source; the legislation.gov.au record was not read).
- Usual period: the contribution must be **received by the fund** within 7 business days after payday — [Superguide](https://www.superguide.com.au/super-booster/payday-super-for-business-transition); [GESB](https://www.gesb.wa.gov.au/employers/super-news/news-and-updates-articles/get-ready-for-payday-super); ATO "making super payments" page (first round, via search text).
- Extended period: **20 business days** after payday for (a) a new employee's first payday and (b) the next payday after an employee chooses a new fund. Later paydays go back to 7 business days. — [Superguide](https://www.superguide.com.au/super-booster/payday-super-for-business-transition); [Workstem "20-Business-Day Rule"](https://www.workstem.com/au/?p=3242); [Mercer](https://www.mercersuper.com.au/employers/learn/payday-super-changes). Sources also disagree on whether the count starts at payday or the day after. — [Access Group glossary](https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-au/payroll/payday-super/payday-super-glossary-employer-guide/) vs Superguide.
- Out-of-cycle payments such as bonuses: super is due 7 business days after the employee's **next regular payday** — [HESTA FAQs](https://www.hesta.com.au/employers/your-responsibilities/payday-super/payday-super-faqs).
- New SGC is assessed per QE day and has 4 components: individual final shortfall; individual notional earnings (GIC rate × base shortfall, compounding daily); an administrative uplift starting at 60% of shortfalls plus notional earnings, which can be reduced by voluntary disclosure; and a 25% choice loading where contributions went to the wrong fund. The core components are deductible. GIC on an unpaid assessment and late-payment penalties are not. — [ATO: the new super guarantee charge](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/payday-super/missed-or-late-payday-super-payments/the-new-super-guarantee-charge) (via search text); [PKF](https://www.pkf.com.au/insights/payday-super-what-does-the-new-legislation-mean-for-employers/); [Kidmans Partners](https://kidmanspartners.com.au/payday-super-the-details).
- SG base is now **qualifying earnings** (QE), replacing OTE alone — [Employment Hero blog](https://employmenthero.com/blog/prepare-for-payday-super/); [Mercer](https://www.mercersuper.com.au/employers/learn/payday-super-changes).
- SG rate is 12% from 1 July 2025, the final scheduled increase; Payday Super does not change it — [ATO small business newsroom](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/small-business-newsroom/the-final-sg-rate-increase-is-coming-on-1-july) (first round).
- SBSCH **closed permanently on 1 July 2026**. Payments sent to it on or after that date are returned. Super for April–June 2026 was still due 28 July 2026 by another payment method. — [ATO "The SBSCH has closed permanently" (2 Jul 2026)](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-and-super-professionals/for-tax-professionals/tax-professionals-newsroom/the-sbsch-has-closed-permanently); [ATO SBSCH page](https://www.ato.gov.au/Business/Super-for-employers/Paying-super-contributions/How-to-pay-super/Small-Business-Superannuation-Clearing-House/); [AustralianSuper](https://www.australiansuper.com/employers/employers-articles/2026/01/ato-sbsch-closure).
- ATO compliance approach for 1 Jul 2026–30 Jun 2027: a PCG under which the ATO will not review "low risk" employers. The PCG number was still not found. — first-round note ([ATO media centre](https://www.ato.gov.au/media-centre/ato-calls-on-employers-to-prepare-for-payday-super)).
- **Xero:** calculates super in each pay run and lets the user review, approve and pay it in one place. Payments route through Xero's SuperStream clearing house (Beam), debiting a nominated bank account, and the pay-period selector moves from quarter to pay period. A Xero product-ideas request asks for super to leave the bank on the scheduled pay-run date rather than the approval date, which suggests timing is driven by approval. — [Xero Payday Super](https://www.xero.com/au/initiative/payday-super); [Xero blog](https://blog.xero.com/au/news-events/payday-super-why-the-right-payroll-platform-matters/); [Trinity Group](https://trinitygroup.com.au/post/process-automatic-superannuation-payments/); [Xero product idea](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967118-payroll-expenses/suggestions/46506967-au-payroll-schedule-superannuation-payments).
- **MYOB:** MYOB Pay Super, an integrated clearing house through SuperChoice; Payday Super readiness changes shipped to MYOB Acumatica in May–June 2026 — [BusinessHub](https://businesshub.com.au/erp/myob-acumatica-payday-super-2026); [pay.com.au + MYOB](https://help.pay.com.au/hc/en-au/articles/17291491558671-How-to-pay-super-using-MYOB-and-pay-com-au).
- **Employment Hero / KeyPay:** HeroClear, an embedded clearing and validation engine built for Payday Super that validates, pays and reconciles super on every pay run, including over the NPP — [Employment Hero help: HeroClear](https://help.employmenthero.com/hc/en-au/articles/17426654964239-What-are-HeroClear-Payday-Super); [CFOtech](https://cfotech.com.au/story/employment-hero-tests-real-time-super-ahead-of-2026).

### Inferences
- **Recommended decision (confirmed):** create one SUPER_PAYABLE open item per pay run. Set `due_date` = payday + 7 business days, or + 20 business days where the payroll provider flags a new employee's first pay or a new fund choice. Out-of-cycle pay runs get a due date of next regular payday + 7 business days. Post the payment as Dr SUPER_PAYABLE / Cr bank, or Cr PAYROLL_CLEARING when the provider's clearing service debits the bank. Show an overdue-super alert on the payables summary. Take the SG amount from the payroll provider on the QE basis and never compute it as 12% × gross. Use national business days excluding weekends and public holidays. How state public holidays are treated is unverified.
- Because the test is *receipt* by the fund, the alert should warn several days before the due date, for example a configurable "clearing lead time" of 2–3 business days. That figure is a design judgement, not a sourced rule.
- Add an SGC_EXPENSE account and an SGC_PAYABLE liability for late payments. GIC on an unpaid SGC assessment should map to a separate non-deductible account. Tax deductibility is a tax-layer flag, not a GL rule.
- The tenant should choose whether the business day count starts at payday or the day after. The safe default is to count from payday, giving the earlier deadline, until the ATO wording is read.

### Gaps
- The exact ATO and legislative wording for the extended period ("20 business days" and its start point) has not been read from the ATO or the Act. Most secondary sources say 20 *business* days.
- The PCG number for the 2026–27 compliance approach was not found.
- How business days are defined (national or state holidays) under the Act is unverified.
- Details of the Payday Super Regulations 2026 are unverified.

## D2. Depreciation in MVP (AASB 116): accumulated-depreciation and expense roles, capitalisation threshold, recurring SL/DV journal; register with disposals in Phase 1; tax depreciation off-ledger

### Takeaway
**CONFIRMED.** AASB 116 requires systematic depreciation over useful life whatever the tax treatment. The capitalisation threshold is an entity policy; no AASB figure exists. Established AU SMB software works in tiers. MYOB Business does not calculate depreciation and expects a general journal. QBO has a register only on its Advanced tier, with book depreciation only. Xero Fixed Assets has a book depreciation register plus an AU tax book for ATO pools. A recurring depreciation journal in MVP, with a register and disposals in Phase 1, therefore matches or beats market practice for the target segment. Keeping tax depreciation off the GL is also market practice. The **$20,000 instant asset write-off from 1 July 2026** is still not cleanly confirmed. The bill passed both houses on 19 Aug 2026, and the ATO page (updated 27 Aug 2026) reportedly says "now law", but no source gives a Royal Assent date.

### Cited Findings
- AASB 116 recognition, depreciation and derecognition rules (para 7 recognition, para 9 aggregation, para 43 components, paras 50–62 depreciation, paras 67–72 derecognition) — see first-round note; paragraph numbers are partly unverified (aasb.gov.au blocked).
- A capitalisation threshold is set by entity policy. Example: Cairns Regional Council uses $5,000 for plant and equipment and $10,000 for other assets — [Cairns Asset Capitalisation Policy](https://www.cairns.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0017/9152/LIVE-674076-v10-Asset_Capitalisation_General_Policy.pdf); [ANU non-current assets overview](https://services.anu.edu.au/financial-management/assets/accounting-for-non-current-assets-overview). No authoritative SMB-specific threshold found.
- **MYOB Business:** "While MYOB doesn't calculate depreciation automatically, you can quickly record your depreciation figures using a general journal entry." AccountRight has no asset register; MYOB staff point users to add-ons or Excel. Exo and Acumatica have full fixed-asset modules. — [MYOB support: about depreciation accounts](https://www.myob.com/au/support/myob-business/accounting/about-depreciation-accounts); [MYOB community](https://myob.lithium.com/discussions/tax_bas_gst_eofy/assets-register/635840); [MYOB Advanced FA overview](https://help.myob.com.au/advanced/Docs/Published/UserGuide/FA__FA_Features.html).
- **QuickBooks Online AU:** fixed assets only in QBO Advanced. It records monthly depreciation automatically (straight-line, 150%, double-declining) and has no separate ATO tax book. The "no tax book" claim comes from a competing vendor. — [QuickBooks AU blog](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/au/blog/product-update/how-to-simplify-fixed-asset-mamagement-using-accounting-software/); [AssetAccountant QBO AU](https://www.asset.accountant/blog/quickbooks-online-vs-assetaccountant-australia/).
- **Xero Fixed Assets:** book depreciation register; for AU and NZ, Xero provides a tax depreciation book (ATO pools in AU). Users still ask for separate book/tax pool schedules. — [AssetAccountant: Xero Fixed Assets](https://www.asset.accountant/blog/xero-fixed-asset-management/) (competing vendor); [Xero product idea: depreciation pool schedules](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967133-reports-tax/suggestions/44988556-fixed-assets-depreciation-pool-schedules). The Xero Central help page was not read.
- **IAWO $20,000:** law to 30 June 2026 — [ATO $20,000 IAWO](https://www.ato.gov.au/about-ato/new-legislation/in-detail/businesses/20000-dollars-instant-asset-write-off). The 2026–27 Budget (12 May 2026) proposed making it permanent from 1 July 2026 — [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/tax/instant-asset-write-off-permanent-extension-chalmers-budget/). The enabling bill was reported as "Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 2) Bill 2026" and passed both houses on 19 Aug 2026 — [Aspire](https://aspireapp.com/en-au/blog/instant-asset-write-off-rules); [Boxas](https://www.boxas.com.au/resources/guides/instant-asset-write-off-2026/). Search text reports the ATO page (updated 27 Aug 2026) as saying the measure "is now law" — [ATO $20,000 IAWO](https://www.ato.gov.au/about-ato/new-legislation/in-detail/businesses/20000-dollars-instant-asset-write-off). Conflicting sources: Aspire (September 2026) says it was awaiting Royal Assent; a 26 Aug article says it was not law; [Spry Roughley](https://news.spryroughley.com.au/insights/20000-instant-asset-write-off-now-permanent-for-small-business) (9 Sep) says it has passed. Without the change, the threshold for 2026–27 would revert to $1,000. ASBFEO commented on a separate "Tax Reform No. 1 Bill 2026" in June 2026 — [ASBFEO submission](https://asbfeo.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-06/260609%20SB%20-%20Economics%20Legislation%20Committee%20consultation%20on%20Treasury%20Laws%20Amendment%20%28Tax%20Reform%20No.%201%29%20Bill%202026%20and%20a%20related%20bill.pdf).
- Small business pool: 15% in the first year, then 30%. The lock-out rule was suspended to 30 June 2026. — first round ([ATO simpler depreciation](https://www.ato.gov.au/business/depreciation-and-capital-expenses-and-allowances/simpler-depreciation-for-small-business/)).

### Inferences
- **Recommended decision (confirmed):** in MVP, provide ACCUM_DEPRECIATION (per asset class) and DEPRECIATION_EXPENSE roles, a tenant `capitalisation_threshold` setting (no default mandated; suggest an editable default such as $1,000 ex GST, clearly labelled as policy), and a recurring SL/DV depreciation journal posted through the posting engine. In Phase 1, add an asset register with per-asset schedules, annual review of useful life and residual value, and disposals with gain or loss and GST on sale. Keep tax depreciation (IAWO, small business pool) **off the GL**. At most, store a tax-treatment field on the asset for the tax agent's export. The capitalisation threshold must never default to the IAWO limit, because that limit is a tax concession, not an AASB materiality policy.
- Making IAWO status a parameter (threshold by income year) avoids hard-coding a law whose status is uncertain.

### Gaps
- Royal Assent date and Act number for the permanent $20,000 IAWO were not confirmed. The bill's exact title ("Tax Reform No. 2") is also unconfirmed against APH or legislation.gov.au.
- Xero Central's description of its book and tax depreciation was not read directly; the claims come from a competing vendor.
- No authoritative source found for a "typical" SMB capitalisation threshold.

## D3. Accruals and prepayments with auto-reversal

### Takeaway
**CONFIRMED.** The accrual basis is mandatory for general-purpose financial statements other than cash-flow information. Today this sits in AASB 101 para 27 (from prior knowledge; not fetched). Under AASB 18, which applies to for-profit annual periods from 1 Jan 2027, AASB 108 is retitled "Basis of Preparation of Financial Statements" and receives content moved from AASB 101. That the accrual-basis paragraph moves with it is likely but unverified. Xero offers an "auto-reverse on date" on manual journals. MYOB's community suggests AccountRight lacks auto-reversal, which is unverified. An auto-reverse flag plus accrued-expense and prepayment roles with schedules matches or exceeds market practice.

### Cited Findings
- AASB 18 renames AASB 108 "Basis of Preparation of Financial Statements" and moves content from AASB 101 into it, including going concern — [Grant Thornton TA 2024-9](https://www.grantthornton.com.au/insights/technical-alerts/ta-2024-9/); [PKF](https://www.pkf.com.au/insights/aasb-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/); [BDO Tier 2 alignment with AASB 18](https://www.bdo.com.au/en-au/blogs/corporate-reporting/aasb-proposes-to-align-the-tier-2-financial-statements-presentation-with-aasb-18). Effective 1 Jan 2027 for for-profit entities — from prior knowledge, not confirmed in results.
- Xero manual journals have an auto-reverse date. A reversal date cannot be added after posting. Editing an auto-reversing journal does not update its reversal. — [Xero product ideas: add auto-reverse date to posted journals](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967127-practice-tools/suggestions/50500908-manual-journals-suggestion-to-add-auto-reverse-d); [Xero product ideas: editing journal with auto-reversal](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967133-reports-tax/suggestions/44960791-manual-journals-editing-journal-with-auto-revers); [Xero product ideas: repeating journals option to auto-reverse](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967133-reports-tax/suggestions/46396369-repeating-journals-option-to-auto-reverse), which implies repeating journals lack auto-reverse.
- MYOB AccountRight: a community idea titled "Journal entry – automatic reversal of journal" contrasts AccountRight with Xero's auto-reverse box — [MYOB community idea](https://myob.lithium.com/idea/accountrightideas/journal-entry-automatic-reversal-of-journal/251987/comments/551949) (suggests the feature is absent; unverified).
- Standard accrual pattern: date the accrual in the period the service was consumed and the reversal at the start of the next period — [Sage community](https://communityhub.sage.com/gb/sage-50-accounts/f/financial-year-end/260474/reversing-journals).
- Accruals sit within trade and other payables; provisions are distinguished by uncertainty — IAS 37 para 11 (first round).

### Inferences
- **Recommended decision (confirmed):** add an `auto_reverse_on` date to manual and system journals. The reversal is a new posted journal linked by `reversal_of_id`, which keeps posted entries immutable. Also add ACCRUED_EXPENSES (liability) and PREPAYMENTS (asset) roles, and prepayment release schedules that mirror the deferred-revenue engine. Two improvements over Xero, both based on gaps users report: (a) allow auto-reversal on recurring journals; (b) since posted journals are immutable, "editing" an accrual means reversing it and reposting, and the linked auto-reversal is regenerated with it.
- Also cover accrued wages and accrued super where a pay period straddles period end.

### Gaps
- AASB 101 para 27 text and the AASB 18 / AASB 108 destination paragraph were not read.
- Whether MYOB Business or AccountRight and QBO AU support auto-reversing journals was not confirmed from official help.

## D4. Leave provisions (AASB 119) and leases (AASB 16)

### Takeaway
**CONFIRMED.** LSL is an "other long-term employee benefit" under AASB 119, measured at present value (discounted at high-quality corporate bond rates in AU). Annual leave is short-term if expected to be settled wholly within 12 months, otherwise long-term. For the current/non-current split under AASB 101 para 69(d), entitlements the entity cannot defer (vested LSL, typically after about 7 years, and annual leave) are presented as **current even if not expected to be settled within 12 months**. Conditional (unvested) LSL is non-current. AASB 16 offers two *optional* recognition exemptions: short-term leases (12 months or less, no purchase option, elected by asset class) and low-value assets (lease by lease, value when new; BC100 refers to about US$5,000). **Tier 2 (AASB 1060) gives disclosure relief only. It gives no recognition or measurement relief**, though AASB 1060 does disapply some AASB 16 disclosure paragraphs.

### Cited Findings
- LSL is an "other long-term employee benefit". The obligation arises as service is rendered even when unvested. Measurement is the present value of estimated future cash outflows for service to the reporting date. — [NSW Treasury TPG24-23](https://www.treasury.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-07/tpg24-23-accounting-for-long-service-leave.pdf); [NSW TC21-03](https://treasury.nsw.gov.au/index.php/documents/tc21-03-accounting-long-service-leave-and-annual-leave); [AASB 119 compiled](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/node/248).
- Current/non-current split: LSL for 7 or more years of continuous service is shown as current even where not expected to be wholly settled within 12 months, because the entity has no (unconditional) right to defer settlement. Conditional LSL is generally non-current. — [NSW TC15-09](https://www.treasury.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/pdf/TC15-09_Accounting_for_Long_Service_Leave_and_Annual_Leave.pdf); [Western Parkland City Authority note 12](https://www.wpca.sydney/about-us/the-western-parkland-city-authority/annual-reports/annual-report-2020-2021/finance/consolidated-financial-statements/12-current-non-current-liabilities-provisions/). The 2020 IASB/AASB amendment removed "unconditional" from para 69(d): the right to defer must exist at the reporting date and have substance — [Moore Australia](https://www.moore-australia.com.au/news/changes-to-current-non-current-classification-of-loans-and-other-liabilities/).
- Short-term vs long-term annual leave; G100 corporate bond rates for discounting — first-round note ([Nexia](https://nexia.com.au/node/199); [BDO](https://www.bdo.com.au/en-au/blogs/corporate-reporting/december-2022/corporate-bond-rates-for-long-service-leave-calculations-available-for-purchase-from-the-g100)).
- AASB 16 low-value election is lease by lease (AASB 16 para 8 per search text). BC100 refers to underlying assets worth "US$5,000 or less" when new. "Low value" is not defined in the standard. Value is assessed when new, so a used photocopier fails. — [KPMG AU AASB 16 Check](https://kpmg.com/au/en/home/insights/2019/04/aasb-16-check-short-term-low-value-exemptions.print.html); [Better Regulation BC98–BC104](https://service.betterregulation.com/document/427999); [NSW Treasury AASB 16 guidance](https://www.treasury.nsw.gov.au/documents/guidance-aasb-16-leases-new-lease-standards).
- Tier 2: AASB 1060 requires all recognition and measurement requirements — [KPMG Farewell SPFS](https://kpmg.com/au/en/insights/financial-reporting/farewell-spfs-welcome-simplified-disclosures.html) (first round). AASB 1060 states that AASB 16 paras 51–60, 60A, 89–92 and B48–B52 do not apply to entities applying AASB 1060, which is disclosure relief — [AASB 1060 BC / AASB 2020-9](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-08/AASB1060_03-20_BC_2020-9.pdf) (via search text).
- Public sector uses AUD 10,000 as the low-value benchmark; this does not bind private entities — [Finance RMG 110](https://www.finance.gov.au/government/managing-commonwealth-resources/accounting-leases-rmg-110/identifying-lease) (first round).

### Inferences
- **Recommended decision (confirmed, with one refinement):** in Phase 1, provide ANNUAL_LEAVE_PROVISION (current) and LSL_PROVISION_CURRENT / LSL_PROVISION_NON_CURRENT roles. Import balances from the payroll provider and post the movement each period. **Refinement:** classify by *vesting / right to defer* (vested LSL → current), not by expected settlement within 12 months. Expected settlement drives *measurement* (short-term undiscounted vs long-term discounted). Classification drives *presentation*. The import must therefore carry years of service, or a vested flag, per employee. LSL present value: a shorthand calculator (probability of reaching vesting × salary growth × discount factor at the G100 corporate bond rate) or a provider-supplied figure. Leave on-costs (super, payroll tax, workers comp) should be an optional percentage uplift; the super-on-leave QE treatment is unverified.
- AASB 16: default policy elects both exemptions and expenses straight-line via bills or recurring journals. Record the election in tenant accounting-policy settings, and suggest a low-value prompt at about AUD 7,500–8,000 as a *guide*. This is a design judgement converting US$5,000; it is not authoritative. The Phase 2 manual path covers ROU asset / lease liability opening, liability amortisation by the effective-interest method, and ROU depreciation through the depreciation engine. Tier 2 does not change this.

### Gaps
- AASB 119 paragraph numbers and AASB 101 para 69 current text were not read from aasb.gov.au.
- Whether super (QE) is payable on leave paid out or taken, for on-cost purposes, is not researched.
- State LSL vesting periods vary by jurisdiction; not researched.
- Whether many SMB (non-reporting) entities prepare special-purpose statements that may depart from AASB 16/119 is not determined. The ledger should support compliance regardless.
