The 12 principles

  1. Every number is traceableOne click from any figure to the documents and journal behind it.
  2. Status is a word plus a colourNever colour alone. Every chip has text and an icon.
  3. Money is set for readingRight-aligned, tabular figures, 2 decimals, negatives in (parentheses) announced as “minus”.
  4. Show the journal before you postPreview mode is one click on every postable document, open by default in reviews.
  5. Nothing posted is deletedVoid says “creates reversal journal”; delete is shown as unavailable, with the reason.
  6. Controls explain themselvesSoD, thresholds and lock dates appear as sentences before you act, not errors after.
  7. Two-person actions show both peopleMaker, checker and you — by name, role and time.
  8. Weakened controls are visibleSmall-team mode and overrides are labelled, reported and acknowledged.
  9. Sync is never hiddenSalesforce status on every synced record and in the top bar; books never wait for it.
  10. Keyboard-first entryEvery list and form works without a mouse; shortcuts are shown where they apply.
  11. Calm densityComfortable by default for owners, Compact for bookkeepers — same columns.
  12. Plain words first“Customers owe you” on top, “Accounts receivable” underneath. One primary action per view.
