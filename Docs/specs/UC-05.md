## Actors / roles
AP Clerk, Approver, supplier (emails PDF)

## Preconditions
- Supplier party with Supplier role.
- Bills inbox address configured.
- Open period for AP.

## Trigger
Supplier PDF emailed to the bills inbox, or bill entered manually.

## Main flow
1. Inbound email becomes a draft bill with the PDF attached.
2. AP Clerk codes lines (or accepts suggestions from past bills of the supplier).
3. Bill routes to an Approver.
4. On approval the bill posts (GP-008) and creates an AP open item.

## Alternate and failure flows
- A1 Entered-by = approver above $1,000 → blocked (small-team mode: external reviewer or recorded exception).
- A2 Bill dated in AP-locked period → rejected unless override.
- A3 Duplicate supplier invoice number → warning, cannot post twice.

## Business rules
- §3.8 row 6; §6.5 rule 3; §3.5.

## Data touched (LedgerForce)
bill, bill_line, attachment, journal_*, subledger_entry

## Salesforce fields touched
None.

## Postings produced
- GP-008 (see [postings.md](../accounting/postings.md); DRAFT until approved)

## Security / SoD checks
- AP Clerk cannot approve own bills; Approver cannot create bills they approve (§6.4).

### SS-UC05-01 — Emailed PDF becomes a draft bill
- Phase: MVP · Layer: **db-integration** · Screenshot evidence: no · Task(s): T-0051
- **Given** a PDF emailed to the bills inbox
- **When** ingest runs
- **Then** a draft bill exists with the PDF attached and nothing posted

### SS-UC05-02 — Suggested coding from past bills
- Phase: MVP · Layer: **unit** · Screenshot evidence: no · Task(s): T-0050
- **Given** a supplier whose last 3 bills used Office expenses / GST
- **When** a new bill is coded
- **Then** the suggestion is Office expenses with tax code GST

### SS-UC05-03 — Coded bill routes to Approver; nothing posts before approval
- Phase: MVP · Layer: **db-integration** · Screenshot evidence: no · Task(s): T-0052
- **Given** a coded bill
- **When** the AP Clerk submits it
- **Then** it is Awaiting approval in the Approver queue and no journal exists

### SS-UC05-04 — Approved bill posts GP-008
- Phase: MVP · Layer: **golden** · Screenshot evidence: no · Task(s): T-0050, T-0052
- **Given** BILL-000045 as in GP-008
- **When** the Approver approves it
- **Then** the journal equals GP-008 and the AP open item is 2,750.00

### SS-UC05-05 — SoD: clerk cannot approve own bill above $1,000
- Phase: MVP · Layer: **db-integration** · Screenshot evidence: no · Task(s): T-0052, T-0015
- **Given** a $2,750 bill entered by user A
- **When** user A approves it
- **Then** approval is denied with SOD_BILL_SELF_APPROVAL and audited

### SS-UC05-06 — Small-team mode records a visible exception
- Phase: MVP · Layer: **db-integration** · Screenshot evidence: no · Task(s): T-0015, T-0052
- **Given** small-team mode and a single user
- **When** the user approves their own bill
- **Then** approval succeeds only with a recorded exception that appears on the weekly review report

### SS-UC05-07 — Bill in AP-locked period rejected

- **Then** posting is rejected with PERIOD_LOCKED
