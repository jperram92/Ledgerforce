## Actors / roles
Scheduler, Billing To contact, AR Clerk, Account owner in Salesforce

## Preconditions
- Reminder schedule configured (step 1 at 7 days).
- Invoice posted and unpaid.

## Trigger
Daily reminder schedule run.

## Main flow
1. Find invoices due + 7 days unpaid without reminder 1.
2. Email reminder 1 to Billing To (MVP).
3. Write LF_Credit_Status__c = "Overdue" on the Account (MVP).
4. Log a Salesforce Task on the Account for the owner (Phase 1).
5. At 30 days place the party on credit hold, blocking auto-approve; visible in Salesforce (Phase 1).

## Alternate and failure flows
- A1 Invoice paid before run → no reminder.
- A2 Re-run same day → no duplicate reminder.

## Business rules
- UC-04; §5.4 party.credit_hold_changed; §5.5 Task (optional).

## Data touched (LedgerForce)
reminder_schedule, reminder_log, party.credit_status, outbox

## Salesforce fields touched
Write: Account.LF_Credit_Status__c, Task (Phase 1).

## Postings produced
- None.

## Security / SoD checks
- Run as system job; AR Clerk can pause reminders per party (audited).
