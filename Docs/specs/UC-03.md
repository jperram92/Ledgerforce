## Actors / roles
Customer (pays), Stripe (webhooks, payouts), AR Clerk (manual receipts), Integration user

## Preconditions
- Invoice posted and emailed with a Stripe payment link (§5.7).
- Stripe connector configured with clearing and fee accounts (postings Q2).
- Webhook endpoint signing secret stored encrypted.

## Trigger
Stripe webhook (payment succeeded) or a bank-transfer receipt recorded/matched.

## Main flow
1. Webhook signature and timestamp verified; event id used as idempotency key (§6.9).
2. Receipt posts gross into Stripe clearing and allocates to the invoice (GP-003).
3. Invoice status Paid; outbox payment.received/invoice.paid.
4. Write-back: LF_Invoice__c Paid + payment date, Opportunity LF_Amount_Paid__c, Account LF_Balance_Due__c and LF_Last_Payment_Date__c.
5. At payout, net settlement posts bank net, fee and GST on fee against clearing (GP-004); bank line matches in UC-08.

## Alternate and failure flows
- A1 Bank transfer → receipt recorded or created from bank matching (GP-002).
- A2 Partial payment → invoice Partially paid.
- A3 Duplicate webhook → ignored.
- A4 Invalid signature/old timestamp → 400, nothing posted.
- A5 Payout dated in AR-locked period → period matrix decides (§11.3 item 4, open).

## Business rules
- §3.8 rows 2–3; §5.4 write-back fields; §5.6.1 idempotency.
