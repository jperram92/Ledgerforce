## Actors / roles
Salesforce sales user (trigger), Integration (service) user, AR Clerk, Approver (above threshold)

## Preconditions
- Salesforce org connected (§5.2) and Opportunity chosen as invoice trigger in setup (§4.1 step 5; default is an open decision §11.2).
- Account synced as a Party with Customer role and a Billing To contact (§4.2).
- Products synced and mapped to product posting profiles; party profile mapped (§4.4); no gap for the combination.
- Tenant has a Standard invoice template and an INV numbering series (§4.3, §3.4).

## Trigger
CDC change event: Opportunity.StageName → Closed Won (or Order activated where configured).

## Main flow
1. Connector receives the CDC event, de-duplicates by idempotency key (record Id + commit number) (§5.6.1).
2. Billing source is resolved: Opportunity + OpportunityLineItems → invoice lines via the tenant template (§4.3).
3. Each line resolves product → product profile; Account → party profile; matrix gives revenue account and tax code (§4.4).
4. Draft invoice is created with dimensions defaulted from party/product/Salesforce fields (§2 dimensions).
5. Outbox event invoice.drafted → write-back sets Opportunity.LF_Invoice_Status__c = "Draft" within 60 s (§5.4).
6. If auto-approve is on and total ≤ threshold and party not on credit hold: invoice is approved, posted (GP-001 shape), numbered, PDF rendered and emailed to Billing To/CC; status written back.

## Alternate and failure flows
- A1 No Billing To contact → draft created, approval blocked with reason; shows in AR worklist.
- A2 Posting-profile gap → draft flagged with the gap; nothing posts; gap appears in the gap-check list (§4.4).
- A3 Duplicate or replayed event → no second draft (§5.6.1).
- A4 Close date in a locked period → tenant lock-date policy: reject to Sync issues, or post to first open date with a note (§2, §5.6.5).
- A5 Salesforce unavailable for write-back → invoice still drafts/posts; write-back queued and retried (§5.6.4).
- A6 Total above auto-approve threshold → remains Draft awaiting AR Clerk/Approver.

## Business rules
- One owner per field: Opportunity data is Salesforce-owned; LF_ fields are LedgerForce-owned (§5.5, D1).
- Posting only through the engine (§3.9).
- Tax per line half-up (§3.4).
- Invoice cannot be approved without Billing To (§4.2).

## Data touched (LedgerForce)
party, party_contact, product, invoice, invoice_line, journal_entry/line, subledger_entry, account_balance, outbox, sync_inbound_log

## Salesforce fields touched
Read: Opportunity (StageName, CloseDate, Amount, AccountId, Owner), OpportunityLineItem, Product2.Family, Account.Type. Write: Opportunity.LF_Invoice_Status__c, LF_Amount_Invoiced__c; LF_Invoice__c (upsert by LF_Id__c); Account.LF_Balance_Due__c.

## Postings produced
- GP-001 (see [postings.md](../accounting/postings.md); DRAFT until approved)
- GP-018 (see [postings.md](../accounting/postings.md); DRAFT until approved)

## Security / SoD checks
- Integration user scopes only (§6.4).
- Auto-approve is a tenant setting changeable only by Finance Admin; audited.
- Credit-hold check before auto-approve (Phase 1 hold).

## Success scenarios

Test layers: unit · property · golden · db-integration · contract · e2e-playwright. Each test is named with its SS id prefix. Status is tracked only in the

### SS-UC01-01 — Closed Won creates a draft invoice from the template
- Phase: MVP · Layer: **db-integration** · Screenshot evidence: no · Task(s): T-0069
- **Given** an Opportunity with 2 line items (Professional services $1,000, Subscription $250) on an Account with a Billing To contact
- **When** the Opportunity moves to Closed Won
- **Then** exactly one Draft invoice exists with 2 lines mapped to the PROFESSIONAL_SERVICES and SUBSCRIPTION profiles and tax code GST

### SS-UC01-02 — Salesforce shows "Draft" within 60 seconds
- Phase: MVP · Layer: **e2e-playwright** · Screenshot evidence: **yes** · Task(s): T-0069, T-0067
- **Given** SS-UC01-01 has run against a scratch org
- **When** 60 seconds elapse
- **Then** Opportunity.LF_Invoice_Status__c = "Draft" (screenshot of the Opportunity record)

### SS-UC01-03 — Auto-approve under threshold posts GP-001 and emails

### SS-UC01-08 — Closed Won dated in a hard-locked period follows lock policy

- **Then** the journal is committed and the write-back stays queued, then succeeds on recovery with one upsert
