## Actors / roles
AR Clerk, Approver (above threshold), customer

## Preconditions
- Invoice INV-000123 posted and paid.

## Trigger
AR Clerk issues a partial credit note.

## Main flow
1. Credit note reverses revenue and GST proportionally (GP-005).
2. Credit held as customer credit.
3. Refund pays out via Stripe or the next payment batch (GP-006).
4. Salesforce shows net amount paid.

## Alternate and failure flows
- A1 Credit above invoice creditable amount → rejected.
- A2 Clerk approves own credit note above threshold → denied.

## Business rules
- §3.8 row 4; §6.4 AR Clerk limits.

## Data touched (LedgerForce)
credit_note, refund, allocation, journal_*

## Salesforce fields touched
Write: LF_Invoice__c credited, Opportunity.LF_Amount_Paid__c (net), Account balance.

## Postings produced
- GP-005 (see [postings.md](../accounting/postings.md); DRAFT until approved)
- GP-006 (see [postings.md](../accounting/postings.md); DRAFT until approved)

## Security / SoD checks
- Approval above threshold by another user.
